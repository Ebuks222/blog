---
title: "8 Months After Abduction: Anambra Police Arrest Five Suspects, Recover
  Kidnap Victim’s Remains and ₦5m Ransom"
date: 2026-11-15T18:37:00.000+01:00
---
The months-long search for Chinedu Echezona, who was abducted in January 2026, has ended in tragedy following the arrest of five suspected members of a kidnapping gang by the Anambra State Police Command.



The suspects, identified as Chidozie Francis, 24; Kosisọchukwu Eziani, 28; Akachukwu Maduabuchi, 22; Chibuzor Onurah, 29; and Echezona Ude, 22, were arrested on November 12, 2026, at Omoba Phase II, Nkpor, Onitsha, following sustained intelligence-led investigations by operatives of the Rapid Response Squad (RRS), Awkuzu.



The operation also led to the recovery of the victim’s remains, his black Honda CR-V Jeep with registration number YAB 220 DM, and the sum of ₦5 million reportedly collected as ransom.



According to the police, Echezona was kidnapped in the early hours of January 29, 2026, with his abductors initially demanding ₦20 million for his release.



Despite initial investigative efforts, including operations that extended to a neighbouring state, the case remained unresolved for several months.



However, a breakthrough reportedly came following further intelligence gathering and investigations by the RRS operatives, culminating in the arrest of the five suspects.



During interrogation, the suspects allegedly confessed to their involvement in the abduction, disclosing that they collected ₦5 million from the victim’s family before killing him.



They also reportedly revealed that two firearms were used during the operation.



In a disturbing twist, the suspects allegedly provided information on where the victim’s remains had been concealed. Acting on the information, police operatives, at about noon on November 14, led the suspects to the location and successfully recovered the remains.



The remains have since been deposited in a mortuary for preservation and necessary forensic examination.



The development represents a significant breakthrough in a case that had remained unresolved for months, raising renewed attention to the dangers posed by kidnapping syndicates and the devastating consequences of ransom-related crimes.



Reacting to the development, the Commissioner of Police, Anambra State Command, CP Nnanna Oji Ama, psc, mnips, directed operatives to intensify efforts to recover the two firearms allegedly used in the crime and establish the full circumstances surrounding the victim’s abduction and murder.



The CP commended the operatives for their persistence, professionalism and determination in pursuing the case despite the considerable time that had elapsed since the incident.



He also reaffirmed the Command’s commitment to dismantling criminal networks, strengthening intelligence-driven policing and ensuring that those responsible for violent crimes are brought to justice.



The Anambra State Police Command assured residents of its continued efforts to protect lives and property, urging members of the public to support security agencies with timely and credible information.
