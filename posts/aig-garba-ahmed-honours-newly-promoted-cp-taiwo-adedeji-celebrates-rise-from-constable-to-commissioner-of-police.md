---
title: AIG Garba Ahmed Honours Newly Promoted CP Taiwo Adedeji, Celebrates Rise
  from Constable to Commissioner of Police
date: 2026-07-30T10:04:00.000+01:00
---
The Assistant Inspector-General of Police in charge of Zone One Headquarters, Kano, AIG Garba Ahmed, has honoured the newly promoted Commissioner of Police, CP Taiwo Adedeji Abdul Mojeed, at a colourful send-forth ceremony in recognition of his outstanding service and remarkable career progression within the Nigeria Police Force.



The event, held at the Zone One Headquarters in Kano, brought together senior police officers, rank-and-file personnel, and friends who paid glowing tributes to CP Adedeji for his exemplary leadership, professionalism, humility, and unwavering commitment to duty during his tenure as Deputy Commissioner of Police in charge of the Zonal Criminal Investigation Department (ZCID).



Speaking at the ceremony, AIG Garba Ahmed described the occasion as one of the happiest moments of his career, expressing pride in witnessing CP Adedeji's elevation to the rank of Commissioner of Police. He praised the newly promoted officer for his loyalty, dedication, and spirit of teamwork, noting that he remained dependable throughout the period they worked together.



The AIG also commended the Heads of Departments at the Zone for their cooperation and professionalism, attributing the Zone's operational success to the strong working relationship among officers.



In his response, CP Taiwo Adedeji expressed gratitude to Almighty Allah for the privilege of rising through the ranks from Constable to Commissioner of Police. He also appreciated his parents for their prayers and sacrifices and thanked AIG Garba Ahmed for his mentorship, guidance, and unwavering support, which he described as pivotal to his professional journey.



The ceremony featured goodwill messages from senior officers, colleagues, and friends, who congratulated the newly promoted Commissioner of Police and wished him continued success in his new responsibilities.



The event concluded with the presentation of a commemorative gift by AIG Garba Ahmed to CP Taiwo Adedeji, followed by group photographs marking the memorable occasion.
