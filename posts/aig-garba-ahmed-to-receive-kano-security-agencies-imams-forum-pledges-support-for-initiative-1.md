---
title: AIG Garba Ahmed to Receive Kano Security Agencies Imams Forum, Pledges
  Support for Initiative
date: 2026-09-10T22:55:00.000+01:00
---
The Assistant Inspector-General of Police, Zone One Headquarters, Kano, AIG Garba Ahmed, is expected to receive the Kano State Security Agencies Imams Forum on a courtesy visit to the Zone.



The Police Public Relations Officer (PPRO) of the Zone, who will be present during the engagement, is expected to highlight the significance of the visit as the delegation, led by the Chief Imam of the Kano State Police Command, CSP Abdulkadir Haruna, comprises Imams representing the Nigeria Police Force, Armed Forces and other federal security agencies operating in Kano State.



During the visit, CSP Abdulkadir Haruna is expected to express appreciation to the AIG for granting the Forum an audience, stating that the delegation will be at the Zone Headquarters to seek his guidance, support and counsel.



He is also expected to explain that the idea of establishing the Forum was conceived following a Qur'anic competition organised by the Imams Forum in Katsina State. According to him, the Forum was established to strengthen synergy among religious leaders within the security community, provide mutual support, and contribute to addressing public concerns and perceptions about security agencies.



Responding to the delegation, AIG Garba Ahmed is expected to warmly welcome the Forum and describe the visit as one of the most significant engagements he has received throughout his years of service in the Nigeria Police Force.



The AIG is expected to describe the Forum as a strategic and unique initiative capable of making meaningful contributions to promoting professionalism, discipline and ethical conduct among security personnel. He will stress that misconduct and excesses by security personnel are contrary to the teachings and values of Islam.



AIG Garba Ahmed is also expected to encourage the Forum to engage relevant government authorities and organise regular symposiums and enlightenment programmes aimed at admonishing and guiding officers on professionalism, integrity, respect for citizens and the fear of God in the discharge of their duties.



He is expected to assure the delegation of his openness, guidance and maximum support, while urging other heads of security agencies to similarly embrace and support the initiative.



The visit is expected to end with the Forum expressing appreciation to the AIG for his warm reception, guidance and commitment to supporting the objectives of the Kano State Security Agencies Imams Forum.
