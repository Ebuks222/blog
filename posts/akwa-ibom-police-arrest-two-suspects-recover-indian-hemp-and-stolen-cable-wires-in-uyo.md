---
title: Akwa Ibom Police Arrest Two Suspects, Recover Indian Hemp and Stolen
  Cable Wires in Uyo
date: 2026-10-19T18:32:00.000+01:00
---
By Vivian Onwuasoanya 



The Akwa Ibom State Police Command has arrested two suspects in separate operations in Uyo, recovering a large quantity of weeds suspected to be Indian hemp and cable wires believed to be linked to an alleged theft.



The arrests, which occurred on October 14, 2026, form part of the command’s intensified efforts to combat illicit drug activities, tackle theft and strengthen public safety across the state.



In a statement issued on Friday, October 16, the Police Public Relations Officer, DSP Timfon John, said the operations were carried out by police operatives following credible intelligence and a distress call.



According to the statement, operatives acting on intelligence about the alleged sale of illicit drugs along Aka Itiam Street, Uyo Local Government Area, mobilised to the area at about 9:30 p.m. and arrested a suspect identified as Gideon David.



A search conducted on the suspect reportedly led to the recovery of a large quantity of weeds suspected to be Indian hemp.



The police said the suspect remains in custody as investigations continue to determine the extent of his alleged involvement and identify other individuals who may be connected to the suspected drug-related activity.



In a separate incident earlier that day, at about 2:57 p.m., detectives were dispatched to a company located at Udoduoma, near Tropicana, Uyo, following a distress call received through the police control room over an alleged theft.



The operatives subsequently arrested another suspect, identified as Godwin Paulinus, in connection with the incident.



Cable wires were reportedly recovered from the suspect’s bag during a search. The police said investigations were ongoing to establish the circumstances surrounding the alleged theft and determine whether other individuals were involved.



The Commissioner of Police, Akwa Ibom State Command, CP Baba Mohammed Azare, fsi, reaffirmed the command’s commitment to the directives of the Inspector-General of Police and its operational strategy targeting criminal hideouts, illicit drug networks and other activities threatening public safety.



The command described the arrests as part of its intelligence-led policing efforts to disrupt the distribution of illicit substances, prevent crime and protect lives and property across the state.



It added that intensified operations targeting criminal black spots and illicit drug activities were continuing, while discreet investigations were underway to identify and apprehend other suspects who might be connected to the offences.



The commissioner urged residents to remain vigilant and report suspicious movements, drug-related activities and other criminal conduct to the nearest police station or through established police reporting channels.



He assured the public that credible information provided to the police would be treated confidentially and acted upon professionally.



The command reiterated its determination to sustain peace, strengthen public confidence in the police and ensure that Akwa Ibom State remains safe for residents, visitors and legitimate businesses.
