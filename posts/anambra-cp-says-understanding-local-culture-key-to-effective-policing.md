---
title: Anambra CP Says Understanding Local Culture Key to Effective Policing
date: 2026-09-05T22:18:00.000+01:00
---
The Commissioner of Police, Anambra State Command, Nnanna Oji Ama, has stressed the importance of understanding and respecting the culture, traditions and values of the people in achieving effective and people-centred policing in the state.



Nnanna Oji Ama made the assertion on Thursday, September 3, 2026, at the Command Headquarters in Awka, when he received the Commissioner for Information and Value Reorientation, Dr. Law Mefor, and the Commissioner for Culture, Entertainment and Tourism, Mr. Udoji Amedu, on a courtesy visit. The Police Public Relations Officer, SP Ikenga Tochukwu, disclosed this in a statement.



The Commissioner said effective policing goes beyond law enforcement, explaining that a proper understanding of the cultural heritage, traditions, values and peculiarities of the communities would help the Police serve and protect the people more effectively.



He noted that understanding the people and their way of life would strengthen police-community relations, improve public confidence and promote better cooperation between security personnel and residents.



Nnanna Oji Ama also called for sustained collaboration among the Police, government ministries, traditional institutions, community leaders and other relevant stakeholders to address security challenges and promote sustainable peace across Anambra State.



He reaffirmed the Command’s commitment to an open-door policing policy and strategic partnerships designed to deepen trust, communication and cooperation between the Police and the people.



The visiting Commissioners commended the Police Command for its efforts in maintaining public safety and enhancing security across the state. They pledged continued collaboration with the Police in promoting peace and security, positive value reorientation and the preservation of Anambra’s cultural heritage.
