---
title: Anambra CP Strengthens Security Collaboration with 3-3 Estate Chairmen,
  Non-Indigenes, Provides WhatsApp Line for Swift Response
date: 2026-10-19T18:26:00.000+01:00
---
By Vivian Chinwe Onwuasoanya. 



The Anambra State Police Command has intensified its community policing strategy by strengthening security collaboration with estate leaders and non-indigene residents, urging the public to report suspicious activities promptly to enhance crime prevention across the state.



The Commissioner of Police, CP Nnanna Oji Ama, psc, mnips, gave the assurance on Monday, October 19, 2026, while hosting the Association of All Chairmen of 3-3 Estate and the Association of Non-Indigenes in Anambra State. The development was disclosed in a statement issued by the Police Public Relations Officer, SP Tochukwu Ikenga.



According to the statement, the meeting focused on addressing security concerns, improving communication between residents and the police, and identifying areas of cooperation to strengthen public safety and prevent criminal activities.



Speaking during the engagement, CP Nnanna urged residents, irrespective of their ethnic, cultural or geographical backgrounds, to remain security-conscious and promptly report suspicious movements and activities to the police.



He also encouraged estate chairmen, community leaders and association members to mobilise their respective communities to provide credible and timely information that could assist security operatives in preventing crime and responding effectively to emerging threats.



The police commissioner reassured non-indigenes living and conducting legitimate businesses in Anambra State of the Command’s commitment to protecting their lives and property, upholding the rule of law and ensuring their safety.



As part of efforts to improve communication and facilitate swift police intervention, CP Nnanna reiterated the availability of a dedicated WhatsApp security line, 09169531886, through which residents can report security concerns, provide credible information and request police assistance.



He urged members of the public to use the channel responsibly and provide accurate information to enable the Command to respond promptly and take appropriate action.



The Anambra State Police Command called on estate associations, non-indigene groups, community leaders and other residents to sustain their cooperation with security agencies, emphasising that public vigilance, timely information-sharing and collective responsibility remain essential to preventing crime and maintaining peace across the state.
