---
title: Anambra Police Targets Zero Extortion as CP Nnanna  Tightens Security
  Monitoring
date: 2026-08-16T12:35:00.000+01:00
---
For Ndi Anambra, the message from the Police Command is becoming clearer: security must not only be about protecting lives and property, but also about protecting the dignity and rights of the people.



In a strategic move aimed at restoring greater confidence in policing, the Commissioner of Police, Anambra State Command, Nnanna Oji Ama, has strengthened the Command’s Operational Control Rooms to closely monitor police checkpoints, patrol teams and other operational activities across the state.



The initiative follows feedback gathered during recent Anambra Security Stakeholders’ Interactive Sessions held across the three Senatorial Districts.



At the heart of the new strategy is a simple but important objective—zero tolerance for extortion, harassment and other forms of police misconduct.



Under the arrangement, the Command’s Operational Control Rooms will maintain closer oversight of patrol formations and checkpoints, while Area Commanders, Divisional Police Officers and Tactical Team Commanders will be held accountable for personnel deployed under their supervision.



Complaints and reports concerning extortion, harassment or unprofessional conduct are expected to be received, monitored and escalated for appropriate action.



This development offers a fresh sense of hope for residents who want a police force that is not only strong against criminals but also professional in its relationship with the people it serves.



For Anambra, where commercial activity, transportation and daily movement remain vital to the state’s economy, effective and accountable policing is essential. Residents should be able to travel, trade and go about their legitimate businesses without fear of criminal attacks—or unlawful harassment.



Oji Ama has made it clear that while the Command remains committed to protecting Ndi Anambra and other residents, no police officer will be allowed to abuse the authority entrusted to him or her by the Nigeria Police Force.



The Commissioner has directed all Area Commanders, Divisional Police Officers and Tactical Team Commanders to ensure strict compliance with the directive, warning that personnel found engaging in extortion or other misconduct will face appropriate disciplinary action in accordance with the law and established regulations.



The development also places an important responsibility on the public.



Residents are encouraged not to remain silent when confronted with misconduct. Credible incidents of extortion, harassment or other unprofessional behaviour can be reported through the Command’s Operational Control Room on 07039194332, the Police Public Relations Officer on 08039334002, or the Commissioner of Police’s dedicated WhatsApp line on 09169531886.



The success of this initiative, however, will depend on both sides—the Police and the people.



The Police must continue to demonstrate professionalism, transparency and accountability, while members of the public must provide credible information and cooperate with security personnel.



Beyond arrests and security operations, this approach represents an important step toward preventive policing—identifying problems early, responding to complaints quickly and addressing misconduct before it damages public trust.



For Ndi Anambra, there is reason to hope that stronger monitoring, better accountability and closer engagement between the Police and communities can contribute to a safer state.



A police officer who knows that his actions are being monitored is more likely to remain professional. A citizen who knows that genuine complaints will be heard is more likely to trust and cooperate with the Police.



And that trust can become one of the most powerful weapons against insecurity.



With the Command’s renewed focus on operational monitoring and zero tolerance for extortion, Anambra can look forward to a policing system where security, accountability and respect for the people go hand in hand.



The journey toward a safer Anambra is a collective one, but initiatives such as this provide residents with renewed confidence that prevention, professionalism and accountability can become central pillars of security in the state.
