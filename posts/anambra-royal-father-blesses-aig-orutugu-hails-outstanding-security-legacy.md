---
title: Anambra Royal Father Blesses AIG Orutugu, Hails Outstanding Security Legacy
date: 2026-08-03T22:21:00.000+01:00
---
The blessings and heartfelt prayers offered by His Royal Majesty, Igwe Gibson Nwosu, OON, carried profound significance, reflecting the deep respect and appreciation AIG Ikioye Orutugu earned through his exceptional service to Anambra State. Throughout his tenure as Commissioner of Police, AIG Orutugu distinguished himself with courageous leadership, professionalism, and a firm commitment to combating crime, restoring public confidence, and strengthening collaboration between the Police, traditional institutions, and local communities. His impactful stewardship left a lasting legacy of improved security and earned him the admiration of the people and traditional rulers across the state.

In blessing the AIG and praying for greater success in his new role, the revered monarch acknowledged not only his well-deserved promotion but also the remarkable achievements he recorded in Anambra. The royal father's prayers symbolized the gratitude of the Awka Kingdom and served as a testament to the enduring relationship AIG Orutugu built with the people through dedicated and selfless service.
