---
title: Argungu's Vision for Police Discipline
date: 2026-08-03T10:20:00.000+01:00
---
Discipline remains one of the most important pillars of effective policing. A police force that is guided by professionalism, accountability, and integrity is better equipped to earn public confidence, enforce the law fairly, and safeguard the lives and property of citizens. This is why the role of the Police Service Commission (PSC) has become increasingly significant in strengthening standards within the Nigeria Police Force.

As the constitutional body responsible for the appointment, promotion, discipline, and dismissal of police officers—except the Inspector-General of Police—the Police Service Commission serves as a vital institution in ensuring that professionalism and ethical conduct remain the hallmark of policing in Nigeria.

Under the leadership of DIG Hashimu Argungu (Rtd.), Chairman of the Police Service Commission, the Commission has continued to emphasise discipline, accountability, and adherence to due process in the discharge of its constitutional responsibilities. His leadership reflects a firm belief that professionalism must remain the foundation of effective policing and that officers entrusted with enforcing the law must also be held to the highest ethical standards.

Chairman Argungu has consistently advocated a police institution where discipline is not viewed as punishment alone, but as a core value that promotes excellence, responsibility, and respect for the rule of law. His commitment to strengthening institutional accountability aligns with the broader objective of building a police force that enjoys the trust and confidence of the Nigerian people.

The Commission's disciplinary mandate plays a crucial role in preserving the integrity of the Nigeria Police Force. By ensuring that allegations of misconduct are addressed through established procedures while recognising officers who demonstrate exemplary service, the PSC contributes to the development of a more professional, motivated, and service-oriented police institution.

Beyond discipline, the Commission's oversight of promotions and appointments continues to reinforce merit, competence, and fairness within the Force. These responsibilities are essential to maintaining morale, encouraging dedication to duty, and ensuring that deserving officers are recognised for their commitment to public service.

As policing continues to evolve in response to emerging security challenges, the need for a disciplined, accountable, and professional police force has never been greater. The Police Service Commission, under the chairmanship of DIG Hashimu Argungu (Rtd.), continues to play an important role in advancing these ideals through its oversight functions and commitment to institutional excellence.

The pursuit of discipline is not merely an administrative responsibility; it is a cornerstone of justice, public confidence, and effective law enforcement. By promoting accountability, professionalism, and ethical leadership, the Police Service Commission is contributing to the continued growth of a modern Nigeria Police Force capable of meeting the expectations of the people it serves.

A disciplined police force is the foundation of a safer society, and the continued emphasis on integrity and accountability by the Police Service Commission under Chairman Hashimu Argungu offers renewed hope for a stronger, more professional, and more trusted policing system in Nigeria.
