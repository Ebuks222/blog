title: Bayelsa CP Conducts Surprise Inspection of Five Police Divisions, Warns
  Officers Against Unlawful Arrests
date: 2026-10-19T16:03:00.000+01:00
---
By Vivian Onwuasoanya



The Commissioner of Police in Bayelsa State, CP Ajao Saka Adewale, has conducted an unannounced inspection of five police divisions across Ogbia and Nembe Local Government Areas, charging officers to maintain professionalism, remain alert and strictly adhere to police regulations.



The surprise duty tour, conducted on Sunday, October 18, 2026, was aimed at assessing personnel discipline, operational readiness, sanitation, the state of police facilities and compliance with established policing standards.



The divisions inspected were Otuokpoti, Kolo and Ogbia in Ogbia Local Government Area, as well as Nembe Ogbolomabiri and Nembe Bassambiri in Nembe Local Government Area.



During the visits, CP Adewale inspected station facilities, detention areas and duty posts to evaluate the preparedness of officers and the general condition of the police formations.



He also interacted with personnel on the demands of their duties, emphasising the need for constant alertness, effective supervision and conduct that inspires public confidence in the Nigeria Police Force.



The police commissioner specifically charged Divisional Police Officers (DPOs) to ensure that their stations remain clean, orderly and functional, while strengthening supervision to promote discipline and efficiency among personnel.



He stressed that professionalism must be reflected not only in the physical condition of police facilities but also in officers' interactions with members of the public.
