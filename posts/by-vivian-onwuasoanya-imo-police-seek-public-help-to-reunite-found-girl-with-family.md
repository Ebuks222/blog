The child was found at Umuoma, Nekede, in Owerri West LGA, on October 10, 2026, as police appeal to parents, relatives and members of the public for information.



The Imo State Police Command has appealed to members of the public to help identify and reunite a female child found at Umuoma, Nekede, in Owerri West Local Government Area of the state, with her family.



The child was reportedly found on October 10, 2026, and is currently the subject of a public appeal by the police to establish her identity and locate her parents or guardians.



In a statement, the Command urged parents, guardians, relatives and anyone with useful information about the child or her family to visit Nekede Police Station to assist in the process of reuniting her with her loved ones.



The police expressed appreciation for the cooperation of members of the public, stressing the importance of collective efforts in protecting vulnerable persons, particularly children.



The Command also reiterated its commitment to safeguarding lives and ensuring the protection of vulnerable members of society.



The statement was signed by the Police Public Relations Officer of the Imo State Command, DSP Okoye Henry, ANIPR, who called for public cooperation to ensure the child's safe reunion with her family.



Members of the public with relevant information are encouraged to report to Nekede Police Station to assist the police in tracing the child's family.



Anyone with useful information is urged to come forward to help reunite the child with her loved ones.
