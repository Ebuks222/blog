The Army Chief also expressed appreciation to President Bola Ahmed Tinubu for his continued support for the Nigerian Army, noting that the expansion of the divisional structure will significantly enhance border security, improve the speed of troop deployment, strengthen operational effectiveness, and reinforce the Army's capacity to protect Nigeria's sovereignty and ensure the safety of its citizens.



In a related development, the Theatre Commander of Joint Task Force (North East), Operation HADIN KAI (OPHK), Major General Abdulsalam Abubakar, has reaffirmed the operation’s commitment to restoring lasting peace and security across the North East, stating that ongoing military efforts are focused on creating a safe environment for socio-economic activities to thrive.



Major General Abubakar, while receiving a media delegation, welcomed the visit and reiterated the command’s readiness to support professional, balanced and objective media coverage of ongoing counter-terrorism and counter-insurgency operations in the region. He stressed that accurate reporting remains critical to sustaining public trust and strengthening national unity.



He further emphasised the importance of strategic communication in military operations, particularly in countering misinformation, disinformation and propaganda, while ensuring that factual accounts of the activities of the Armed Forces of Nigeria are properly projected. According to him, the integration of kinetic and non-kinetic operations, intelligence-driven missions, and sustained disruption of terrorist logistics networks has continued to yield significant operational gains.



The Theatre Commander noted that these efforts have enhanced the protection of vulnerable communities, facilitated the return of thousands of internally displaced persons to their ancestral homes, improved security for farming and other economic activities, led to the recovery of arms and combat equipment, and compelled the surrender of several terrorist commanders, fighters and logistics collaborators, marking steady progress in the overall stabilisation of the North East region.
