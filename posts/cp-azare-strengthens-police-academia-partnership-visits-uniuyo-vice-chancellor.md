---
title: CP Azare Strengthens Police-Academia Partnership, Visits UNIUYO Vice Chancellor
date: 2026-09-01T23:59:00.000+01:00
---
By vivian Onwuasoanya 



The Commissioner of Police, Akwa Ibom State Command, CP Baba Mohammed Azare, psc, has reaffirmed the Command’s commitment to strengthening campus security and deepening collaboration between the Nigeria Police Force and the academic community.



CP Azare stated this during a courtesy visit to the Vice Chancellor of the University of Uyo (UNIUYO), Prof. Christopher Ekong, as part of his ongoing strategic engagements with key stakeholders across the state.



The visit was aimed at strengthening cooperation, improving information sharing and enhancing security around the university community, including students, lecturers, administrative staff and visitors.



During the engagement, the Commissioner emphasized the importance of a strong and sustained partnership between the Police and academic institutions, noting that universities play a critical role in human capital development and the socio-economic advancement of society.



He assured the university management of the Command’s commitment to protecting lives and property within and around the institution, stressing that the Police would continue to work closely with university authorities, students, staff and other security stakeholders to prevent crime, respond effectively to security concerns and maintain peace and order.



CP Azare also called for proactive and intelligence-led policing, effective communication and the early identification of emerging security threats. He emphasized that security remains a collective responsibility and urged the university community to provide timely information on suspicious activities to the appropriate security authorities.



The Commissioner further assured the university of the Command’s readiness to provide necessary security support for academic activities and other legitimate engagements within the institution.



Responding, the Vice Chancellor, Prof. Christopher Ekong, welcomed CP Azare and commended the Police Command for its efforts toward maintaining peace and security in Akwa Ibom State.



Prof. Ekong stressed that a secure academic environment is essential for effective teaching, learning, research and institutional development. He expressed the university management’s readiness to strengthen its partnership with the Police and other security agencies, particularly in the areas of information sharing, crime prevention, student safety and peacebuilding.



The engagement further reinforced the shared commitment of the Police Command and the University of Uyo to creating a safe and secure academic environment where students, lecturers, researchers and other members of the university community can carry out their legitimate activities without fear.



The Akwa Ibom State Police Command reiterated its commitment to professional, people-oriented, proactive and intelligence-led policing, while sustaining strategic partnerships with institutions and other stakeholders in its efforts to keep the state safe and secure.
