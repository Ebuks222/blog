---
title: CP Baba Intensifies Security Ahead of Political Activities in Akwa Ibom
date: 2026-09-10T22:59:00.000+01:00
---
The Akwa Ibom State Police Command, under the leadership of the Commissioner of Police, CP Baba Mohammed Azare, has intensified security preparations ahead of increasing political activities across the State, directing all political parties, support groups and associations to formally notify the Police before embarking on rallies, processions, campaigns, meetings or other large public political gatherings.



The directive, contained in a statement signed by the Commissioner of Police, is aimed at strengthening proactive policing, preventing clashes and scheduling conflicts, and ensuring adequate security and traffic management during political activities.



CP Azare has urged organisers to engage the Police well in advance and provide relevant details, including the date, time, venue, expected number of participants and proposed routes where applicable. This, he noted, would enable the Command to properly assess security needs and deploy personnel effectively.



The Commissioner has also issued a strong warning to political thugs, troublemakers and other individuals who may attempt to disrupt the peace of the State. He warned that the Police would take firm and lawful action against anyone involved in violence, thuggery, possession of offensive weapons, intimidation of political opponents, destruction of property or unlawful obstruction of public roads.



The CP further directed Area Commanders, Divisional Police Officers and Tactical Commanders across the State to intensify intelligence gathering and maintain heightened security around political activities and other public gatherings.



The proactive approach of CP Baba Mohammed Azare reflects a strong commitment to preventive policing and the protection of lives and property, particularly as political activities increase. His emphasis on early notification, intelligence-led deployment and strict enforcement of the law demonstrates a clear determination to ensure that political participation is conducted peacefully and without threats to public safety.



The Akwa Ibom State Police Command reaffirmed its commitment to providing a level playing field for all political parties and groups, irrespective of political affiliation, while maintaining law and order throughout the State.



Under CP Azare’s leadership, the Command’s emphasis on dialogue, intelligence gathering, early intervention and professional policing is a commendable step towards sustaining the peace and security for which Akwa Ibom State is known.
