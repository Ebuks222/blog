---
title: CP Bakori Commends Philanthropist for Renovating Gwale Police Division
date: 2026-08-18T22:45:00.000+01:00
---
The Commissioner of Police, Kano State Command, CP Ibrahim Adamu Bakori, has commended Hajiya Hajara Abdullahi of Diso Quarters, Gwale LGA, for sponsoring the total renovation and furnishing of Gwale Police Division.

CP Bakori presented a Letter of Appreciation to the philanthropist on Monday, August 17, 2026, at the Command Headquarters, describing her as a “rare gem” and a committed partner in community policing.

The Commissioner noted that Hajiya Hajara’s continued support for Police formations across Kano State has contributed to improved working conditions for officers and enhanced service delivery to members of the public.

He stressed that support from individuals and organisations directly contributes to crime prevention, boosts police morale and strengthens public confidence in security agencies.

Also speaking, the Special Adviser to the Kano State Governor on Community Policing, Hon. Hajiya Maimuna Umar Sheriff, commended the gesture and urged other well-meaning individuals, corporate organisations and community leaders to emulate Hajiya Hajara.

She reaffirmed the Kano State Government’s commitment to supporting security agencies with logistics, mobility and welfare packages to enhance their effectiveness.

The officials emphasised that sustainable security requires strong collaboration among the government, security agencies and members of the public.

The Kano State Police Command expressed appreciation for the gesture and reaffirmed its commitment to working with stakeholders to sustain peace, law and order across the State.
