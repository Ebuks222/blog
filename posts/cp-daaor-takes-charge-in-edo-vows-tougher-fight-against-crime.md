---
title: CP Daaor Takes Charge in Edo, Vows Tougher Fight Against Crime
date: 2026-08-12T23:33:00.000+01:00
---
The Edo State Police Command has welcomed CP Patrick Daaor, MNIPS, as the 51st Commissioner of Police in the state, following his assumption of duty today.

CP Daaor officially takes over the leadership of the Command from AIG Monday Agbonika, fdc, who has since assumed duty as the Assistant Inspector-General of Police supervising the Edo and Delta State Police Commands.

The Police Public Relations Officer (PPRO), Edo State Command, ASP Eno Ikoedem, ANIPR, disclosed this in a statement, noting that the new Commissioner of Police brings to the Command extensive operational, investigative, administrative, intelligence and international policing experience.

According to the PPRO, CP Patrick Daaor was enlisted into the Nigeria Police Force as a Cadet Assistant Superintendent of Police in 1994. He holds a Bachelor of Arts Degree in History and International Studies from the University of Jos and a Master’s Degree in History and International Studies from the Ignatius Ajuru University of Education, Port Harcourt.

CP Daaor has served in various capacities across several commands and formations, including Bauchi, Adamawa, Ondo, Kaduna, Ekiti, Rivers, Bayelsa, the Federal Capital Territory and Lagos State Commands.

His distinguished career has also included international assignments with the United Nations Mission in Kosovo (UNMIK) and the United Nations Mission in Sudan (UNMIS), further broadening his experience in international peacekeeping and policing.

The PPRO stated that CP Daaor has held several key positions within the Nigeria Police Force, including Team Leader, Force Criminal Investigation Department (FCID), INTERPOL; Area Commander, Choba, Rivers State Command; Assistant Commissioner of Police in charge of Operations, Ekiti State Command; Deputy Commissioner of Police in charge of Administration, Police Staff College, Jos; Deputy Commissioner of Police in charge of International Investigation, INTERPOL; and Deputy Commissioner of Police in charge of the State Criminal Investigation Department (SCID), Bayelsa State Command.

He has also served in various operational and divisional capacities within the Federal Capital Territory Command.

Prior to his appointment as the 51st Commissioner of Police, Edo State Command, CP Daaor served as Commissioner of Police, Administration, Force Intelligence Department, Force Headquarters, Abuja.

According to ASP Ikoedem, the new Commissioner of Police has undergone several specialised professional courses, including Combat Courses in Gwoza; Human Rights Training of Trainers Workshop; UNITAR Course on Understanding Conflict and Conflict Analysis; Tactical Leadership and Command Course; Digital Forensic Awareness Course; Military Observers Course at the Nigerian Army Peacekeeping Centre (NAPKC), Jaji; and the Strategic Leadership and Command Course (SLCC) at the National Institute for Police Studies (NIPS).

Upon assumption of duty, CP Daaor reaffirmed his commitment to professional policing, intelligence-led operations, community partnership, respect for human rights and the sustained fight against crime and criminality in Edo State.

The Commissioner called for the cooperation and support of the people of Edo State, traditional institutions, community leaders, stakeholders and the media, stressing the importance of collective efforts in building a safer and more secure state.

He assured residents of the Command's commitment to protecting lives and property, preventing and tackling crime, strengthening intelligence gathering and ensuring effective policing across the state.

The Edo State Police Command, through its Police Public Relations Officer, ASP Eno Ikoedem, ANIPR, urged members of the public to continue supporting the police with credible information that could assist in preventing crime and maintaining peace and security across the state.
