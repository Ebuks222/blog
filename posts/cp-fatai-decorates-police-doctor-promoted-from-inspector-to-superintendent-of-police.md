---
title: CP  Fatai Decorates Police Doctor Promoted from Inspector to
  Superintendent of Police
date: 2026-07-30T10:01:00.000+01:00
---
The decoration of SP (Dr.) Opeyemi Ojo, following his promotion and conversion from the Inspectorate cadre to the rank of Superintendent of Police, is more than a personal career milestone—it is a reflection of the Nigeria Police Force's growing recognition of professionalism, competence, and specialised expertise in modern policing.

Decorated by the Commissioner of Police, Lagos State Command, CP Tijani Fatai, at the Command Headquarters in Ikeja, the elevation underscores the importance of rewarding excellence and creating opportunities for professionals who contribute significantly to the Force beyond conventional policing duties.

Healthcare remains a vital component of every effective security organisation. Police medical personnel play an indispensable role in ensuring the physical and mental well-being of officers, supporting operational readiness, and delivering quality healthcare services to personnel and their families. Recognising their dedication through career progression not only boosts morale but also encourages other professionals to bring their expertise into public service.

The promotion, made on the recommendation of the Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, and approved by the Police Service Commission, demonstrates the leadership's commitment to a merit-driven system where competence and dedication are acknowledged irrespective of one's area of specialisation.

As the Nigeria Police Force continues its transformation agenda, strengthening professional cadres such as medicine, engineering, forensic science, information technology, psychology, and legal services will remain essential to building a modern, efficient, and people-centred police institution.

SP (Dr.) Opeyemi Ojo's elevation is therefore not just a reward for years of dedicated service; it is a powerful statement that excellence, professionalism, and commitment to duty remain pathways to growth within the Nigeria Police Force. It is a development worthy of commendation and one that should inspire officers across all professional disciplines to continue serving with integrity, diligence, and distinction.
