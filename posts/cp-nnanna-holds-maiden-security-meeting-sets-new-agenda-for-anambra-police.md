---
title: CP Nnanna Holds Maiden Security Meeting, Sets New Agenda for Anambra Police
date: 2026-08-11T20:11:00.000+01:00
---
The Commissioner of Police, Anambra State Command, CP Nnanna Oji Ama, psc, mnips, has set out his operational expectations for commanders across the state, with emphasis on proactive policing, intelligence gathering, accountability and improved community engagement.



CP Nnanna stated this on Tuesday, August 11, 2026, during his maiden security meeting with Area Commanders, Tactical Commanders, Divisional Police Officers (DPOs) and other sectional heads of the Command.



The meeting provided the Commissioner with an opportunity to interact with the strategic officers of the Command and outline his priorities towards strengthening operational effectiveness and enhancing public safety across Anambra State.



According to the Police Public Relations Officer, Anambra State Command, SP Tochukwu Ikenga, the Commissioner charged commanders to take full responsibility for the security of their respective jurisdictions and ensure the effective deployment of personnel and available resources.



CP Nnanna emphasised the need for proactive, intelligence-led and community-oriented policing, urging commanders to remain visible and maintain effective operational control within their areas of responsibility.



He further stated that the performance of strategic officers would be closely assessed, particularly in the areas of traffic management, maintenance of law and order, crime prevention and prompt response to security challenges.



The Commissioner stressed that the security situation and level of public safety within each jurisdiction would remain important indicators in assessing the performance of commanders.



He also recognised officers who have distinguished themselves through exceptional performance, introducing awards and commendations as part of efforts to encourage professionalism, dedication and commitment among personnel.



While rewarding excellence, CP Nnanna warned officers against unprofessional conduct, extortion, abuse of office and other practices capable of undermining public confidence in the Police.



He reiterated that all personnel must operate within the ambit of the law and uphold the highest standards of professionalism, discipline and accountability in the discharge of their duties.



The Commissioner further charged commanders to strengthen collaboration with other security agencies, community stakeholders and members of the public, noting that effective policing requires collective responsibility and the timely sharing of credible intelligence.



He urged the strategic officers to remain committed to improving operational efficiency, enhancing traffic management, preventing crime and sustaining peace and security across Anambra State.



SP Tochukwu Ikenga, while reiterating the Command's commitment to supporting the Commissioner's security agenda, said the meeting ended with a renewed commitment by the strategic officers to strengthen operational efficiency and sustain peace and security across the state.
