---
title: CP Nnanna Oji Ama Assumes Duty in Anambra, Vows Tougher Fight Against Crime
date: 2026-08-10T12:10:00.000+01:00
---
The Anambra State Police Command has a new helmsman as CP Nnanna Oji Ama, psc, mnips, officially assumes duty as the Commissioner of Police, with a pledge to strengthen security, consolidate existing gains and deepen collaboration with communities across the state.

The Command made this known in a press release issued on Monday, August 10, 2026, by the Police Public Relations Officer, SP Ikenga Tochukwu.

CP Nnanna Oji Ama takes over from the immediate past Commissioner of Police, AIG Ikioye Orutugu, fwc, mnips, PhD, who was recently promoted to the rank of Assistant Inspector-General of Police before his retirement from the Nigeria Police Force.

With more than three decades of policing experience, the new Anambra CP brings extensive operational, intelligence, investigative and administrative experience to his new assignment.

Before his deployment to Anambra, CP Oji Ama served as Commissioner of Police, Intelligence and Training, Force Intelligence Department, Force Headquarters, Abuja, having earlier served as Deputy Commissioner of Police in the same department.

A native of Ekata in Edda Local Government Area of Ebonyi State, the new Commissioner holds a B.Sc. (Hons) in Psychology from the University of Jos.

He joined the Nigeria Police Force as a Cadet Superintendent of Police and underwent training at the Police Academy, Wudil, Kano State.

Over the course of his career, he has served in several strategic and sensitive positions covering intelligence, criminal investigation, tactical operations, administration, public relations and command.

His previous assignments include Deputy Commissioner of Police, General Investigation, FCID Abuja; Commanding Officer, No. 44 Police Mobile Force, Force Headquarters, Abuja; Area Commander, Nnewi, Anambra State; Assistant Commissioner of Police, Homicide, FCID; Zonal CID, Zone 9, Umuahia; Commanding Officer, No. 11 PMF, Calabar; Divisional Police Officer, Asokoro and Dutse Alhaji in the FCT; Coordinator of Courses, Police Detective College, Enugu; Commanding Officer, No. 26 PMF, Uyo; Divisional Crime Officer, Sango Otta, Ogun State; and Police Public Relations Officer, Rivers State Police Command, among other strategic appointments.

His professional development has also included several local and international courses, including the Policy, Strategy and Leadership Course at NIPSS, Kuru; Joint Operational Planning Course conducted with the British Military Advisory and Training Team; Incident Site Commander’s Course by the British High Commission College of Policing; Tactical, Operational and Strategic Intelligence Command and Management Course; Advanced Detective Course, as well as various Squadron Commanders and Combat Operations Courses.

The new CP has also been recognised for professional courage and operational effectiveness.

In 2007, he received a Commendation Award for uncommon bravery, courage and gallantry following the recovery of an AK-47 rifle during a shootout with armed robbers in Ikot Ekpene, Akwa Ibom State.

He is a member of the International Association of Chiefs of Police, Nigeria Psychological Association and Nigeria Institute of Police Studies.

A New Security Agenda for Anambra

Upon assuming office, CP Nnanna Oji Ama pledged to consolidate the security gains already recorded in Anambra while strengthening partnerships with other security agencies and critical stakeholders.

He specifically emphasised collaboration with traditional institutions, community leaders, youths, women and other stakeholders, recognising the importance of community participation in effective policing.

The Commissioner assured the people of Anambra State that the Command under his leadership would remain committed to the protection of lives and property, prevention and detection of crime, enforcement of the law and the maintenance of public safety.

He also assured residents, visitors and businesses across the state of the Command's determination to create a safer environment where people can live, work and conduct legitimate businesses without fear.

For Anambra, where security remains a major concern for residents, businesses and visitors, the arrival of a Commissioner with extensive experience in intelligence, investigation and tactical operations comes with significant expectations.

The people will now be watching closely as the new CP translates his wealth of experience into practical security outcomes across the state.

The press release announcing the assumption of duty was issued by SP Ikenga Tochukwu, Police Public Relations Officer, Anambra State Command.
