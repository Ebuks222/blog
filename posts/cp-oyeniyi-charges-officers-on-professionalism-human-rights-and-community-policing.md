---
title: CP Yemi Oyeniyi Reaffirms Commitment to People-Centred Policing
date: 2026-08-04T22:02:00.000+01:00
---
The Commissioner of Police, Delta State Command, CP Yemi Oyeniyi, psc, acipm, mnips, has reiterated his commitment to strengthening professionalism, accountability, and community policing, charging officers of the Command to uphold the highest standards of service in line with the vision of the Inspector-General of Police.

According to the Command's Public Relations Officer, SP Bright Edafe, ANIPR, FCIA, FIPMD, the Commissioner gave the charge during a lecture delivered to officers of the Command at the State Headquarters in Asaba on Tuesday, August 4, 2026.

During the lecture, CP Oyeniyi urged senior officers to mentor younger personnel by instilling discipline, integrity, and professionalism, stressing that effective policing depends on a well-trained and ethically grounded workforce.

He also directed investigating officers to ensure that completed cases are promptly charged to court while cautioning against the unnecessary detention of suspects. The Commissioner reminded officers that civility, respect for human rights, and professionalism in their interactions with members of the public remain fundamental to building public confidence in the Nigeria Police Force.

Reinforcing the principles of community policing, CP Oyeniyi encouraged officers to strengthen partnerships with residents, noting that policing can only succeed when founded on trust, cooperation, and mutual respect between the police and the communities they serve.

The lecture, according to the Police Public Relations Officer, forms part of the Command's ongoing efforts to improve service delivery, enhance professionalism, and align policing strategies with the reform agenda of the Inspector-General of Police aimed at delivering efficient, accountable, and people-centred policing across the country.
