---
title: CP Wilfred Olutokunbo Afolabi Assumes Duty as 35th Commissioner of Police
  in Abia
date: 2026-08-10T16:50:00.000+01:00
---
CP Wilfred Olutokunbo Afolabi has assumed duty as the 35th Commissioner of Police of the Abia State Police Command.

He succeeds AIG Danladi Isa, who has been promoted and redeployed to the Force Criminal Investigation Department (FCID) Annex, Kaduna.

CP Afolabi is a seasoned and accomplished police officer with over three decades of meritorious service in the Nigeria Police Force.

He enlisted into the Force as a Cadet Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP) on May 18, 1992, and has built a distinguished career marked by professionalism, discipline and dedication to duty.

The new Commissioner holds a Bachelor of Science (Hons) degree in Botany from the University of Ilorin, as well as a Postgraduate Diploma and a Master’s degree in Peace and Security Studies from the University of Port Harcourt.

He has also undergone several strategic and tactical training programmes, including the Strategic Leadership and Command Course at the National Institute of Police Studies, Abuja; Tactical Leadership Command Course at the Police Staff College, Jos; Internal Security/Low Intensity Conflict Course at the Armed Forces Command and Staff College, Jaji; and Peacekeeping Negotiation Course at the Kofi Annan International Peacekeeping Training Centre, Ghana.

CP Afolabi has extensive international policing and peacekeeping experience. Between 2004 and 2005, he served as the Pioneer Sector Commander of Sector 2, Nyala, and Pioneer Regional Commander of Southern Darfur under the African Union Mission in Sudan.

During the mission, he was instrumental in establishing operational and administrative structures, setting up police stations in internally displaced persons’ camps and coordinating civil police activities.

Between 2001 and 2002, he served as the Nigeria Contingent Commander and Special Response Unit Officer under the Human Rights Section of the United Nations Mission in Bosnia and Herzegovina, where he contributed to law enforcement reforms, system analysis and training development for local police personnel.

Before his deployment to Abia State, CP Afolabi served as Commissioner of Police, Administration, Training and Development at the Force Headquarters, Abuja.

He previously served as Commissioner of Police, Ondo State Command, where he coordinated administrative and operational activities, strengthened intelligence networks, enhanced investigative efficiency and promoted community policing partnerships aimed at improving public trust.

He also served as Commissioner of Police, Force Equestrian Officer, Mounted Troop, at the Force Headquarters, Abuja, where he supervised mounted police operations, ensured operational readiness and maintained professional standards in crowd control and ceremonial duties.

Between 2021 and 2023, he served as Deputy Commissioner of Police, Administration, Delta State Command; Deputy Commissioner of Police, CID, Katsina State Command; and Deputy Commissioner of Police, Operations, Bayelsa State Command.

In these capacities, he handled high-level administrative responsibilities, supervised criminal investigations, coordinated crime prevention strategies and planned operational activities across the respective commands.

Between 2016 and 2021, he served as Assistant Commissioner of Police, Area Commander in Anambra State Command; Assistant Commissioner of Police, Operations, Imo State Command; and Assistant Commissioner of Police, Intelligence, Abuja.

Between 2014 and 2016, he served as a Divisional Police Officer in several divisions within the Federal Capital Territory and as a Directing Staff at the Police Staff College, Jos, where he trained and mentored senior police officers.

His career has also included several operational assignments, including service as Sector Commander and Second-in-Command in Joint Task Force operations in Warri, Staff Officer at Force Headquarters and Unit Commander of the Police Mobile Force in Plateau State, where he coordinated combat operations, riot control and other specialised duties.

CP Afolabi has participated in several major national and international assignments. He was among the first Nigerian police officers deployed to the African Union Mission in Sudan and later served as the last Contingent Commander of the Nigeria Police Team to the United Nations Mission in Bosnia and Herzegovina.

He also served as an Aide-De-Camp to visiting Heads of State and participated in organising major international events, including the All African Games and Commonwealth Summit.

CP Afolabi is a Fellow of the Peace and Security Studies Department, University of Port Harcourt, and a Justice of the Peace (JP). He is married with children and enjoys travelling and reading.

With his extensive experience in strategic command, intelligence, criminal investigation, tactical operations, administration and international peacekeeping, CP Wilfred Olutokunbo Afolabi assumes leadership of the Abia State Police Command with a wealth of professional experience.

The Abia State Police Command has called for the cooperation and support of stakeholders and the general public as the new Commissioner begins his tenure, stressing the importance of collective efforts in ensuring the safety and security of residents across the state.
