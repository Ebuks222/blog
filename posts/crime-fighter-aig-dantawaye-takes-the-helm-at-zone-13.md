---
title: Crime Fighter AIG Dantawaye Takes the Helm at Zone 13
date: 2026-08-02T10:06:00.000+01:00
---
The newly appointed Assistant Inspector-General of Police in charge of Zone 13 Headquarters, Ukpo, Anambra State, AIG Miller Gajere Dantawaye, psc, has officially assumed duty as the 10th substantive AIG of the Zone, succeeding AIG Godwin Iguh Eze, psc(+), who retired after attaining the mandatory retirement age.

A seasoned police officer with over two decades of distinguished service, AIG Dantawaye brings to Zone 13 a wealth of operational, investigative and administrative experience, having served in several strategic positions across the Nigeria Police Force. He previously served as Commissioner of Police, Kogi State Command, Commissioner of Police, Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Command, and most recently as Assistant Inspector-General of Police, Federal Operations (FEDOPS), Force Headquarters, Abuja.

Widely regarded as an effective operational commander, AIG Dantawaye earned commendation during his tenure as Commissioner of Police, FCT Command, where his proactive, intelligence-driven policing strategy contributed significantly to reducing crime and improving public confidence in policing across the nation's capital. His leadership was marked by swift operational responses, enhanced collaboration with other security agencies and a sustained crackdown on criminal elements.

Similarly, as AIG Federal Operations, he coordinated several nationwide security operations, strengthening the Nigeria Police Force's response to violent crimes and other emerging security threats. His strategic leadership and operational expertise have earned him recognition as one of the Force's finest crime fighters.

Born on October 15, 1971, in Ankpa, Kogi State, AIG Dantawaye hails from Kajuru Local Government Area of Kaduna State. He enlisted into the Nigeria Police Force as a Cadet Assistant Superintendent of Police on May 1, 2000, and holds a Bachelor of Science degree in International Studies.

He has attended several professional and leadership courses, including the Civil-Military Cooperation Course at the National Defence College, the VVIP Protection Course in Israel, the Strategic Leadership and Command Course at the Police Staff College, Jos, the Leadership Course at Shere Hills, Jos, and the Combat Operations Course in Gwoza.

AIG Dantawaye is also a member of Rotary International and the Nigerian Institute of Public Relations (NIPR), reflecting his commitment to professional excellence and community service.

In his maiden address, the new AIG expressed gratitude to the Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, for the confidence reposed in him. He pledged to consolidate on the achievements of his predecessor by promoting professionalism, discipline, intelligence-led policing, respect for human rights and stronger collaboration with sister security agencies and relevant stakeholders in combating crime across Anambra and Enugu States, which make up Zone 13.

He further charged officers and men of the Zone to remain committed to the core values of the Nigeria Police Force while ensuring effective service delivery and the protection of lives and property.

Married to Dr. (Mrs.) Bessie Dantawaye and blessed with four children, AIG Dantawaye enjoys reading, driving and humanitarian service. His appointment is widely seen as a significant boost to policing in Zone 13, given his proven track record of operational excellence, crime reduction and transformational leadership.
