The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) has, over the years, continued to strengthen its public communication and engagement with the media, and one name that stands out in this regard is Dele Oyewale, mnipr, Head of Media and Publicity and Official Spokesperson of the Commission.



A seasoned journalist, strategic communicator and crisis-management professional, Oyewale brings to his role a wealth of experience acquired from years in journalism, including his time with respected publications such as TELL and TEMPO magazines.



Since taking up the responsibility of communicating the activities and mandate of the EFCC to the public, Oyewale has demonstrated remarkable professionalism, accessibility, clarity and consistency. His ability to explain complex issues in a language that the public can understand has contributed significantly to effective public engagement with the Commission.



Beyond issuing statements and addressing the media, he has distinguished himself as a communicator who understands the importance of accuracy, credibility and responsible information management—particularly in the sensitive fight against economic and financial crimes.



His recognition as the Outstanding Federal Government Spokesperson of the Year at the 2025 National Spokespersons Awards organised by the Nigerian Institute of Public Relations (NIPR) further underscores his commitment to excellence in public communication.



Security Connect Media celebrates Dele Oyewale, ACE I, for his professionalism, dedication and outstanding contribution to strategic security communication and public enlightenment.



His exemplary service reminds us that effective security communication is not merely about speaking—it is about informing responsibly, building public trust and giving clarity to the issues that matter.



Congratulations to an exceptional spokesperson and consummate professional.



Security Connect Media salutes you, Dele Oyewale, for excellence in communication and service to the nation.
