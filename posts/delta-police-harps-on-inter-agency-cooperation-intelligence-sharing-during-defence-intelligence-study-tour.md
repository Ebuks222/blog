---
title: Delta Police Harps on Inter-Agency Cooperation, Intelligence Sharing
  During Defence Intelligence Study Tour
date: 2026-08-26T21:14:00.000+01:00
---
The Commissioner of Police, Delta State Command, Yemi John Oyeniyi, has called for stronger cooperation and timely intelligence sharing among security and intelligence agencies to effectively tackle emerging security threats in the country.



The Police Public Relations Officer, Delta State Command, Bright Edafe, disclosed this in a statement on the Nigeria Study Tour of participants of the Advanced Defence Intelligence Officers Course (ADIOC) 16/2026 from the Defence Intelligence College, Karu-Abuja, which took place in Asaba on August 25, 2026.



The study tour, involving 20 participants and senior staff of the Defence Intelligence College, was led by the Commandant, Commodore U.N. Bugaje, and received by Oyeniyi alongside members of the Delta State Police Command Management Team.



Speaking on the theme of the tour, “Strengthening National Resilience Through Integrated, Multi-Agency Coordination and Regional Security Cooperation,” Oyeniyi stressed that security agencies must work closely together, share intelligence promptly and coordinate their responses to security threats.



He noted that the changing nature of contemporary security challenges had made collaboration among security and intelligence agencies indispensable to the protection of lives and property.



The Commissioner also highlighted some of the notable achievements recorded by the Delta State Police Command, drawing practical examples from its operations to demonstrate the impact of intelligence-led policing and inter-agency cooperation in preventing and combating crime.



During an interactive session following the presentation, the participants raised questions on policing, intelligence gathering, crime prevention and security operations. The questions were addressed by the Commissioner and senior officers of the Command.



The visit concluded with the Delta State Police Command reaffirming its commitment to sustained cooperation with other security and intelligence agencies in strengthening security and protecting lives and property across the state.



The study tour provided the participants with practical exposure to security operations and demonstrated the importance of integrated responses, intelligence sharing and coordinated action in addressing contemporary security challenges.
