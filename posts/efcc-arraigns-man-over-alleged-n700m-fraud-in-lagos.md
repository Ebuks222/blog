---
title: EFCC Arraigns Man Over Alleged N700m Fraud in Lagos
date: 2026-09-10T22:43:00.000+01:00
category: "Fraud "
---
The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC), Lagos Zonal Directorate 1, has arraigned a man, Osaretin Osagiede, before the Lagos State High Court sitting in Ikeja over an alleged N700 million fraud.



Osagiede was arraigned on Friday, September 4, 2026, before Justice Olukayode Ogunjobi on a three-count charge bordering on unauthorised access to computer material and receiving stolen property.



According to the EFCC, the defendant allegedly worked with Zacharia Lorshe, who is currently at large, to bypass the two-factor authentication (2FA) security system of Ravenpay user accounts between March 2025 and January 2026.



The prosecution alleged that the action was carried out with the intention of gaining unauthorised access to the bank's data.



One of the charges states that Osagiede and Lorshe allegedly dishonestly received the sum of N700 million belonging to Best Start Micro Finance Bank, while having reason to believe that the funds were stolen.



The alleged offences contravene relevant provisions of the Criminal Law of Lagos State, 2011.



When the charges were read to him, Osagiede pleaded not guilty.



Following his plea, prosecution counsel, H.U. Kofarnaisa, urged the court to remand the defendant in a correctional facility pending the hearing of his bail application.



Justice Ogunjobi subsequently adjourned the matter to September 16, 2026, for hearing of the bail application and ordered that Osagiede be remanded in a correctional facility.



The case underscores the growing threat posed by cyber-enabled financial crimes and the increasing use of digital platforms to perpetrate large-scale financial fraud.
