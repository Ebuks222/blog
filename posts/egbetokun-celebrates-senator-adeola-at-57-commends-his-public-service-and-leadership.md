---
title: Egbetokun Celebrates Senator Adeola at 57, Commends His Public Service
  and Leadership
date: 2026-08-10T17:18:00.000+01:00
---
Retired Inspector-General of Police, Dr. Kayode Adeolu Egbetokun, NPM, Ph.D., has congratulated Distinguished Senator Solomon Olamilekan Adeola, CON, popularly known as YAYI, on his 57th birthday, describing him as a dedicated public servant and purposeful leader committed to grassroots development.

Egbetokun, the 22nd Indigenous Inspector-General of Police, conveyed the congratulatory message in a goodwill statement issued to mark the senator’s birthday.

The retired police chief, on behalf of himself and his family, warmly felicitated with Senator Adeola and commended his years of public service, leadership and commitment to improving the lives of the people.

According to Egbetokun, Senator Adeola’s journey at 57 reflects a sustained commitment to public service, with numerous developmental projects and interventions recorded across communities.

“Your life represents a remarkable journey of dedicated public service, purposeful leadership and unwavering commitment to the development and wellbeing of the people,” Egbetokun stated.

He also prayed for God’s continued preservation of the celebrant, wishing him sound health, abundant wisdom, renewed strength and divine guidance as he continues his public service.

Egbetokun extended his prayers to Senator Adeola’s family, asking for continued blessings, greater capacity for service and the grace to accomplish his aspirations.

He further prayed that the senator’s steps would continually be ordered towards greater heights, expressing optimism that the new chapter in his life would bring greater fulfilment, new opportunities to serve humanity and enduring accomplishments.

The retired IGP also expressed hope that Adeola’s vision for the people would continue to receive divine favour and translate into lasting benefits for present and future generations.

While congratulating the senator on attaining 57, Egbetokun prayed that the new age would usher in greater accomplishments and a successful journey towards the Government House.

“Once again, congratulations and many happy returns!” he added.
