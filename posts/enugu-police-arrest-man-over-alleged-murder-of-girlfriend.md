---
title: Enugu Police Arrest Man Over Alleged Murder of Girlfriend
date: 2026-09-23T12:10:00.000+01:00
---
The Enugu State Police Command has arrested a 30-year-old man, Okpara Chibuike Emmanuel, over the alleged murder of his girlfriend, identified only as Chinyere.



The suspect, who resides in Olympic Layout, One-Day, Enugu, was arrested by operatives of the Awkunanaw Police Division on September 21, 2026, at about 6:30 p.m., after he was reportedly apprehended by an angry mob.



According to the Police Public Relations Officer, SP Daniel Ndukwe, the arrest followed information that Emmanuel had allegedly murdered his girlfriend.



Preliminary investigations revealed that the suspect allegedly killed the woman on September 19 following an argument between them. He was said to have wrapped her remains in a nylon bag and locked them inside the toilet of his apartment.



The Police said the offensive odour from the decomposing body later attracted the attention of neighbours, who reportedly forced the door open and apprehended the suspect before the arrival of Police operatives.



The victim’s remains were evacuated to a mortuary for preservation and autopsy, while efforts are ongoing to establish her full identity and trace her relatives.



During preliminary interrogation, the suspect allegedly confessed to the killing, claiming that the argument that preceded the incident was over allegations concerning who infected the other with Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV).



According to the Police, Emmanuel stated that he struck the deceased on the head with a pestle during the argument, resulting in her death, before allegedly concealing her body in the toilet.



The Commissioner of Police, Enugu State Command, CP Mamman Bitrus Giwa, has ordered the State Criminal Investigation Department to conduct a discreet and thorough investigation into the incident.



The Command said the suspect would be arraigned in court upon completion of investigations.



The CP also advised members of the public, particularly young women, to exercise caution when visiting or spending time with people and to keep trusted relatives or friends informed about their movements.
