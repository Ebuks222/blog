---
title: Enugu Police Chief Sounds the Alarm on Misconduct, Orders Total Professionalism
date: 2026-08-02T11:07:00.000+01:00
---
The Commissioner of Police, Enugu State Command, CP Mamman Bitrus Giwa, has reinforced his administration's commitment to professionalism, accountability and ethical policing, declaring that misconduct, indiscipline and abuse of office will no longer be tolerated within the Command.

Speaking during the Command's Monthly Strategic Conference held at the Senior Police Officers' Mess, Agbani Road, Enugu, CP Giwa delivered a strong message to senior officers, warning that no officer found culpable of misconduct would be protected, regardless of rank or position.

His firm stance comes at a time when public confidence in law enforcement continues to depend not only on the ability of the Police to combat crime but also on the conduct and professionalism of officers in uniform.

Addressing Deputy Commissioners of Police, Assistant Commissioners, Area Commanders, Divisional Police Officers and heads of tactical formations, the Commissioner stressed that discipline, integrity and respect for human rights must remain the bedrock of policing. He charged supervisory officers to closely monitor personnel under their command, noting that effective leadership and supervision are essential to preventing misconduct.

CP Giwa also reiterated key operational directives of the Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, emphasizing intelligence-led policing, proactive crime prevention, professionalism and strict adherence to the rule of law.

He condemned extortion, unlawful detention, corruption, harassment of citizens and every form of abuse of office, describing such acts as unacceptable and detrimental to the image of the Nigeria Police Force. According to him, officers who betray the trust placed in them by the public will face disciplinary measures in accordance with existing police regulations.

Beyond internal discipline, CP Giwa has continued to strengthen the Command's operational capacity through intelligence-driven policing. Since assuming office, the Enugu State Police Command has intensified operations against kidnapping, armed robbery, cultism and other violent crimes, while expanding community engagement and improving security coordination across the state.

Security stakeholders believe the Commissioner's leadership style reflects a determination to build a Command anchored on discipline, transparency and operational excellence. His insistence on accountability is seen as part of broader efforts to restore public trust and promote a policing culture where professionalism takes precedence over impunity.

The strategic conference also provided an opportunity to assess the Command's security architecture, review ongoing operations and develop new strategies for addressing emerging security challenges in Enugu State.

For residents, the message from the Police leadership is unmistakable: officers who uphold professionalism, integrity and respect for the law will continue to receive institutional support, while those whose actions undermine public confidence will be held accountable.

As Enugu State continues to strengthen its security framework, CP Mamman Bitrus Giwa's uncompromising stand on discipline and ethical policing signals a renewed commitment to building a Police Command that is not only effective in fighting crime but also worthy of the confidence and trust of the people it serves.
