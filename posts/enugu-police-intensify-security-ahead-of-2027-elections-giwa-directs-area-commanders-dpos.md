---
title: Enugu Police Intensify Security Ahead of 2027 Elections, Giwa Directs
  Area Commanders, DPOs
date: 2026-10-19T16:51:00.000+01:00
---
By Vivian Onwuasoanya



The Enugu State Police Command has intensified preparations for the 2027 general elections, with the Commissioner of Police, CP Mamman Bitrus Giwa, directing Area Commanders and Divisional Police Officers (DPOs) to strengthen security and ensure adequate protection for political activities across the state.



Giwa gave the directive on October 13, 2026, during a strategic conference with senior officers of the Command at the Senior Police Officers’ Mess, Agbani Road, Enugu.



The meeting followed the recently concluded Conference and Retreat of Senior Police Officers (CARSPO 2026), held in Owerri, Imo State, which focused on developing a Nigeria Police roadmap for the effective management of security during elections.



Expressing concern over recent political developments in Enugu State, the CP charged officers to ensure adequate security coverage for political gatherings, whether formally notified to the Police or not, to prevent security breaches and maintain public order.



He also directed Area Commanders and DPOs to engage political parties within their respective jurisdictions and obtain their campaign schedules to facilitate effective security planning and deployment.

![](/assets/images/491397.jpg)



Emphasising the need for professionalism during the electioneering period, Giwa urged officers to remain neutral, non-partisan and committed to the ethical standards of the Nigeria Police Force, regardless of political interests.



The Commissioner reaffirmed the Command’s commitment to impartial policing and the protection of citizens, particularly during lawful political activities across Enugu State.



Beyond election security, Giwa charged heads of tactical and operational departments, formations and units to strengthen crime-fighting strategies, sustain intelligence gathering and conduct professional raids on identified criminal hideouts within their areas of responsibility.



He stressed the importance of proactive policing, effective intelligence-led operations and coordinated security efforts in addressing emerging threats and preventing criminal activities.



The conference was attended by Deputy Commissioners of Police, Assistant Commissioners of Police, Area Commanders, DPOs and heads of various formations, units and sections of the Command.



The directives underscore the Command’s resolve to strengthen public safety, maintain political neutrality and ensure a secure environment for democratic participation as preparations for the 2027 general elections gather momentum.
