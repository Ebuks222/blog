---
title: FCT POLICE BOSS RALLIES DPOS, TACTICAL COMMANDERS FOR EMBER MONTHS, 2027
  ELECTIONS
date: 2026-10-17T18:46:00.000+01:00
---
The Commissioner of Police, Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Command, CP Ahmed Muhammed Sanusi, PhD, FCAI, has charged Area Commanders, Divisional Police Officers (DPOs) and tactical commanders to intensify intelligence-led operations and proactive policing ahead of the ember months and the 2027 general elections.



Sanusi gave the directive on Thursday, October 15, 2026, during a strategic meeting with senior operational officers of the Command in Abuja, where security strategies were reviewed and operational readiness strengthened to address emerging security threats across the FCT.



The meeting focused on enhancing intelligence gathering, strengthening patrol operations, improving police visibility and ensuring effective coordination among operational units to safeguard residents and visitors during the ember months and the forthcoming electoral period.



The CP directed officers to intensify patrols, surveillance and intelligence gathering to prevent criminal activities, particularly armed robbery, kidnapping, car theft and other violent crimes that could threaten public safety.



He also emphasised the importance of professionalism, discipline, respect for human rights and prompt responses to distress calls, urging officers to remain vigilant, proactive and committed to their responsibilities.



As part of preparations for the 2027 general elections, Sanusi directed Area Commanders and DPOs to strengthen visibility policing, identify criminal hotspots and conduct targeted raids to prevent criminal activities and address emerging security threats.



He further stressed the need for sustained intelligence gathering and strict adherence to established electoral security guidelines to ensure a peaceful, safe and orderly electoral process across the Federal Capital Territory.



Addressing traffic management challenges, the police chief expressed concern over increasing road construction activities within the city centre and their potential impact on traffic flow.



He consequently directed the Command's traffic control departments and divisional traffic officers to improve traffic management, strengthen operational coordination and prevent congestion and obstructions on major roads.



Personnel deployed for traffic duties were also urged to remain courteous and professional while ensuring the smooth movement of vehicles, particularly during peak periods when road construction and increased human and vehicular activities could worsen congestion.



The Command reiterated its commitment to protecting lives and property, maintaining public order and ensuring adequate security across the FCT during the ember months and in the build-up to the 2027 general elections.



Residents were urged to remain security-conscious and promptly report suspicious movements or activities to the nearest police station or through the Command's emergency telephone lines: 08028940883 and 08032003913.



The Command assured residents that cooperation between the police and members of the public remains essential to preventing crime, strengthening public safety and maintaining a secure environment across the nation's capital.
