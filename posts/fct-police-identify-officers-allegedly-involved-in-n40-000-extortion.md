---
title: FCT Police Identify Officers Allegedly Involved in N40,000 Extortion
date: 2026-08-26T21:00:00.000+01:00
---
The Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Police Command has identified officers allegedly involved in the extortion of N40,000 from a resident along the Wuye–Utako Road, near Salem Church, Abuja.



The Command said the development followed a video circulating on social media in which the complainant alleged that police officers extorted the sum from him.



In a statement issued by the FCT Police Public Relations Officer, SP Josephine Adeh, the Command said the officers depicted in the video had been identified and taken into custody to facilitate investigation.



The Command also invited the complainant to appear at the FCT Police Command Headquarters, Abuja, to make an official statement and provide relevant details that would assist the investigation and any subsequent disciplinary action.



The complainant is expected to contact the Police Public Relations Officer through 07038979348 or 08180382808.



The Commissioner of Police, FCT Command, Ahmed Muhammed Sanusi, reiterated the Command's zero-tolerance stance on extortion, abuse of authority, misconduct and other forms of unprofessional conduct by police personnel.



Sanusi assured residents of the Federal Capital Territory that any officer found culpable after due investigation would face appropriate disciplinary and legal action in accordance with extant laws and regulations.



The Commissioner further urged members of the public to report cases of police misconduct and provide relevant information that could assist the Command in investigating such complaints.



He reaffirmed the commitment of the FCT Police Command to accountability, professionalism and respect for the rights of residents, stressing that officers found to have violated the law or professional standards would be held accountable.



The Command encouraged members of the public to cooperate with ongoing investigations by providing credible information and formally reporting incidents of misconduct through the appropriate channels.
