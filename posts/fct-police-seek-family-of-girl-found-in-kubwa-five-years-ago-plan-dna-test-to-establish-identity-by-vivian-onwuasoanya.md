---
title: FCT Police Seek Family of Girl Found in Kubwa Five Years Ago, Plan DNA
  Test to Establish Identity By Vivian Onwuasoanya
date: 2026-10-21T22:45:00.000+01:00
---


![](/assets/images/499978.jpg)

The Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Police Command has appealed to members of the public to help reunite a young girl, reportedly found in Kubwa, Abuja, about five years ago, with her biological family.



The appeal highlights the Command’s efforts to establish the girl’s identity and trace her relatives, demonstrating the importance of sustained police attention to cases involving vulnerable children and family reunification.



According to information released by the Command, the circumstances surrounding the discovery of the girl remain unclear, as the exact location where she was found is currently unknown. She has, however, been cared for since then, while efforts are now being intensified to establish her identity and locate her biological parents or relatives.



As part of the process, the Commissioner of Police has indicated that a DNA test will be conducted to help determine her biological identity and establish possible family connections.



The planned DNA test represents an important step towards resolving questions surrounding the girl’s identity and ensuring that any claim of biological relationship can be properly verified.



The Command’s decision to seek public assistance also underscores the role of community participation in police work. Information from residents and other members of the public can be crucial in resolving cases that may otherwise remain unanswered for years.



By making the case public, the FCT Police Command is creating an opportunity for anyone who recognises the girl or has information about her background to assist investigators in tracing her family.



Although the circumstances of the case remain uncertain, the renewed effort offers hope that the missing links in the girl’s history can be established and that she may eventually be reunited with her loved ones, if they are identified and the relationship is confirmed.



The Police Command therefore urges anyone with credible information about the girl or her biological family to come forward and contact the FCT Police Command.



Beyond the search for her identity, the case highlights the human side of policing: protecting vulnerable children, pursuing answers to unresolved questions and creating opportunities for families to be reunited.



The Command’s appeal also serves as a reminder that public cooperation remains an important part of effective policing, particularly in cases where time, limited information and uncertain circumstances make investigations more challenging.



Members of the public are encouraged to share the appeal responsibly. A single piece of credible information could help investigators uncover the girl’s identity and bring her closer to her biological family.
