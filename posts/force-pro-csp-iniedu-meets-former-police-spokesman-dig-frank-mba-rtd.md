---
title: Force PRO CSP Iniedu Meets Former Police Spokesman DIG Frank Mba Rtd
date: 2026-08-02T10:44:00.000+01:00
---
In a demonstration of humility, respect for institutional history and commitment to professional growth, the Force Public Relations Officer of the Nigeria Police Force, CSP Anietie Okokon Edem Iniedu, paid a courtesy visit to former Force Public Relations Officer and retired Deputy Inspector-General of Police, DIG Frank Mba (Rtd.), Ph.D., mni.

The visit was more than a courtesy call—it was a symbolic recognition of the invaluable role mentorship, experience and institutional memory play in strengthening the Nigeria Police Force. During the meeting, CSP Iniedu expressed profound appreciation for DIG Frank Mba's exceptional leadership, mentorship and enduring contributions to policing, strategic communication and public relations, acknowledging the solid foundation he laid as one of the Force's most accomplished spokespersons.

DIG Frank Mba, widely respected for redefining police public relations through professionalism, transparency and proactive engagement with the media and the public, remains an inspiration to many officers within and outside the Force. His distinguished career was marked by effective crisis communication, strategic public engagement and an unwavering commitment to projecting a positive image of the Nigeria Police Force.

For CSP Iniedu, who recently assumed office as the new Force Public Relations Officer, the visit reflects his leadership philosophy of learning from those who have walked the path before him. It also reinforces his belief that the future of police communication can only be strengthened by building on the knowledge, values and experiences of past leaders.

The meeting further underscores the importance of continuity in leadership and the need to preserve institutional memory, ensuring that the achievements and lessons of retired senior officers continue to guide the next generation of police professionals.

As the Nigeria Police Force continues its transformation towards a more professional, transparent and people-centred institution under the leadership of the Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, gestures such as this reaffirm the value of mentorship, mutual respect and continuous learning in advancing the Force's mission.

As philosopher George Santayana aptly stated, "Those who cannot remember the past are condemned to repeat it." CSP Iniedu's visit to DIG Frank Mba is a testament to the understanding that honouring those who have served with distinction is not merely an act of respect, but a pathway to building a stronger and more effective Nigeria Police Force for generations to come.
