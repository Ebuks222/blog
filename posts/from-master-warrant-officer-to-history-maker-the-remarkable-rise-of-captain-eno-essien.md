---
title: "From Master Warrant Officer to History Maker: The Remarkable Rise of
  Captain Eno Essien"
date: 2026-08-12T10:21:00.000+01:00
---
On April 5, 2026, the Nigerian Army recorded a significant milestone in the history of women in the military with the promotion and decoration of Master Warrant Officer (MWO) Eno Essien (AHQ DAT DCC) to the rank of Captain.

The promotion makes Captain Eno Essien the first female officer in the history of the Nigerian Army to benefit from a Concessional Promotion to the rank of Captain, according to the announcement made during her decoration ceremony.

The historic elevation represents more than a personal career achievement. It is a significant development in the advancement and recognition of women who have continued to contribute to the Nigerian Army through professionalism, discipline and dedication to duty.

For years, women in uniform have continued to demonstrate their capacity to serve and excel across various responsibilities within the nation's security architecture. Captain Essien's achievement adds another important chapter to that story.

Her journey from Master Warrant Officer to Captain reflects a career shaped by commitment, discipline, resilience and service. Her elevation is a recognition of the value of experience and dedication within the military profession.

A Milestone for Women in Uniform

The significance of Captain Essien's promotion extends beyond the rank itself.

As the first female Master Warrant Officer to receive such a Concessional Promotion to Captain, she has become a trailblazer for other women serving in the Nigerian Army and a source of inspiration to young women who aspire to pursue careers in the military.

Her achievement demonstrates that professional excellence and commitment to duty can create opportunities for advancement, regardless of gender.

For women who have had to overcome doubts and stereotypes about their capacity to serve and lead, the milestone sends a powerful message: competence, dedication and professionalism can break barriers.

The achievement also underscores the importance of recognising individuals who have distinguished themselves through years of service and commitment to national duty.

Recognition of Dedication and Service

Captain Essien's promotion is a reminder that military careers are built on discipline, sacrifice and consistency.

Her elevation reflects a record of service worthy of recognition and demonstrates the importance of maintaining professional standards throughout one's career.

Rather than seeking recognition, she allowed her dedication to duty and professional conduct to speak for her. Her new rank therefore represents not only an advancement in responsibility but also an acknowledgement of her contribution and service.

With the new rank comes greater responsibility and expectations. As a Captain of the Nigerian Army, Captain Essien is now positioned to continue contributing her experience and knowledge to the service while serving as an example to those coming behind her.

Inspiring the Next Generation

The significance of having women occupy positions of greater responsibility within the military cannot be underestimated.

Representation provides young women with tangible examples of what can be achieved through determination, education, discipline and hard work.

Captain Essien's story is therefore likely to resonate beyond the barracks. For a young girl who dreams of wearing the military uniform, her achievement provides evidence that such aspirations can become reality.

For female soldiers already serving, it reinforces the message that their efforts and professionalism can lead to new opportunities.

And for the Nigerian Army, the milestone forms part of the broader evolution of women's participation and advancement within the institution.

A Historic Chapter

The April 5 decoration ceremony will remain a notable moment in Captain Eno Essien's career and in the history of women in the Nigerian Army.

Her promotion is not simply about the insignia attached to a new rank. It represents years of service, dedication and perseverance culminating in a moment of historic recognition.

Captain Eno Essien has become the first female officer to achieve this particular milestone through Concessional Promotion to Captain.

In doing so, she has not only advanced her own career but has also expanded the possibilities for those who will follow.

Her story is a testament to the power of perseverance and professional excellence.

Congratulations to Captain Eno Essien on this historic achievement. May her new rank bring greater responsibility, fulfilment and continued distinction in service to the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
