---
title: GIWA SUSTAINS HANDSHAKE PATROL TO ENUGU-BENUE BORDER, CHARGES OFFICERS ON
  PROFESSIONALISM
date: 2026-08-26T23:52:00.000+01:00
---

The Commissioner of Police, Enugu State Command, CP Mamman Bitrus Giwa, has sustained the Command’s interstate Handshake Patrol initiative with a patrol to the Enugu-Benue boundary, as part of efforts to strengthen security collaboration, prevent crime and improve police visibility across the state.

CP Giwa conducted the patrol on Tuesday, August 25, 2026, covering the Udenu area through the Enugu-Opi-Nsukka Road and extending to the Enugu-Benue boundary.

At the boundary point, the Commissioner was received by the Otukpa Area Commander of the Benue State Police Command, ACP Cosmas Eze.

CP Giwa charged ACP Eze and the Udenu Area Commander, ACP Stephen Abiaziem, to sustain the existing interstate operational synergy through the Handshake Patrol initiative of the Nigeria Police Force, introduced by the Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, psc (+), NPM.

He stressed that collaboration between adjoining State Commands remains essential to tackling contemporary and complex crimes, particularly criminal activities that can cross jurisdictional boundaries.

The Commissioner urged both Area Commanders to maximise the opportunities provided by the Handshake Patrol initiative through sustained operational cooperation and intelligence sharing.

As part of the exercise, CP Giwa also visited the Udenu, Umabor and Unity Police Divisions, where he addressed Divisional Police Officers and personnel on the need to maintain professionalism and respect the rights and dignity of members of the public.

He charged officers to ensure courteous and responsible treatment of citizens, noting that the conduct of personnel remains critical to public confidence in the Nigeria Police Force.

At the Enugu-Opi-Nsukka Road, the Commissioner interacted with operatives of the Distress Response Squad, where he warned them against extortion, corruption and other forms of misconduct.

He also addressed Charge Room Officers and other personnel, reminding them that they serve as the first point of contact for many members of the public who visit police stations.

CP Giwa therefore directed them to conduct themselves professionally and ensure that their interactions with citizens reflect the values and standards of the Nigeria Police Force.

The Commissioner said the routine patrols and supervisory visits are in line with the policing vision of IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu and are aimed at strengthening proactive and intelligence-led policing, preventing crime, improving police visibility, boosting public confidence and enhancing professionalism across the Command.

CP Giwa was accompanied on the patrol by the Deputy Commissioner of Police in charge of Operations, DCP Gregory Itobore; the Commanding Officer, No. 3 Police Mobile Force, Enugu, ACP Azeez Idowu; the Commander, Distress Response Squad, ACP Kelvin Aiyegbusi; and the Head of the Command’s Violent Crime Response Unit, CSP Henry Imo.

Also on the patrol were the Head of the Anti-Kidnapping Sub-Unit of the VCRU, CSP Chukwuka Odum; the Commanding Officer, Counter Terrorism Unit, SP Uju Okeke; and the Head of the Anti-Cultism Sub-Unit of the VCRU, DSP Isaac Odogwu, alongside other officers and men.

The Enugu State Police Command said it would continue to sustain proactive patrols, interstate operational collaboration and supervisory engagements as part of efforts to strengthen security and provide effective policing services to residents.
