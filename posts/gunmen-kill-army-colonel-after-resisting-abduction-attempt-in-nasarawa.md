---
title: Gunmen Kill Army Colonel After Resisting Abduction Attempt in Nasarawa
date: 2026-07-29T14:29:00.000+01:00
---
Security Connect News



A serving Nigerian military intelligence officer, Colonel Abdussalam A. Ude, was reportedly killed after resisting an attempted abduction by armed men at his residence in Badna, New Karu Local Government Area of Nasarawa State.



According to information available to Security Connect News, the incident occurred on Monday evening, July 27, 2026, shortly after the senior officer arrived at his residence.



Security sources disclosed that the gunmen ambushed Colonel Ude as he alighted from his vehicle and allegedly attempted to whisk him away. However, the officer reportedly resisted the attack despite being heavily outnumbered, frustrating the kidnappers' efforts.



The assailants were said to have opened fire on the senior military officer, shooting him multiple times at close range. He reportedly died at the scene from the gunshot wounds.



The attackers also allegedly shot members of his household during the incident. His wife, driver and security guard sustained varying degrees of gunshot injuries and were rushed to a medical facility, where they are currently receiving treatment.



Colonel Ude, a native of Igbo-Eze South Local Government Area of Enugu State, was widely regarded as a dedicated intelligence officer. Sources indicated that he was due for promotion to the rank of Brigadier General next year.



An Igbo Muslim, the late officer is scheduled to be buried at the Mambilla Mosque in Abuja in accordance with Islamic rites.



The tragic killing has sparked renewed concerns over the security situation within the Federal Capital Territory and its neighbouring communities, particularly the growing threat of kidnapping and violent attacks targeting residents.



As of the time of filing this report, security agencies had commenced investigations and launched efforts to apprehend those responsible for the attack. No official statement had yet been issued by the Defence Headquarters regarding the incident.



Security Connect News will continue to monitor developments and provide updates as more verified information becomes available.
