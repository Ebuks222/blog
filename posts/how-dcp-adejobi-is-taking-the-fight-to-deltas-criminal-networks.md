---
title: How DCP Adejobi Is Taking the Fight to Delta's Criminal Networks
date: 2026-08-01T21:40:00.000+01:00
---
The Deputy Commissioner of Police in charge of Operations, Delta State Command, DCP Adejobi Olumuyiwa, has continued to distinguish himself through intelligence-driven policing and coordinated operations that are disrupting criminal networks and strengthening security across Delta State.

As the Command's operational lead, DCP Adejobi has spearheaded a series of strategic raids targeting cult groups, armed gangs and other criminal elements in several parts of the state, particularly in Ughelli and other identified flashpoints. These operations have resulted in the arrest of key suspects, the dismantling of criminal hideouts and the restoration of relative peace in communities previously affected by violent crimes.

His proactive approach to policing has earned commendation from security stakeholders, who credit the Operations Unit's swift responses with preventing the escalation of violent clashes and improving public safety. The intelligence-led strategy adopted under his leadership has continued to enhance the Command's capacity to combat crime while reassuring residents of the Police's commitment to protecting lives and property.

Beyond operational achievements, DCP Adejobi has emphasized professionalism, discipline and accountability within the Command. Officers under his supervision are expected to carry out their duties with integrity, impartiality and respect for human rights. He has also demonstrated zero tolerance for misconduct by ensuring that allegations against police personnel are thoroughly investigated and appropriate actions taken where necessary.

As Delta State continues to confront evolving security challenges, DCP Adejobi remains committed to strengthening intelligence gathering, enhancing collaboration with communities and deploying proactive policing strategies aimed at preventing crime before it occurs. His leadership reflects the Nigeria Police Force's renewed commitment to professionalism, public trust and effective service delivery.

A seasoned police officer with extensive operational and administrative experience, DCP Adejobi has served in several strategic capacities within the Nigeria Police Force. He has attended numerous professional courses in criminal investigation, intelligence-led policing, tactical operations and strategic leadership, equipping him with the expertise required to lead complex security operations. His wealth of experience continues to play a significant role in advancing the Delta State Police Command's mission of ensuring a safer and more secure environment for all residents.
