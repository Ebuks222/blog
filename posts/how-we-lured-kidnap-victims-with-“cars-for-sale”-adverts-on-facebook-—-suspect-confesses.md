What I would recommend for your son

Since he is waiting for university, I wouldn't necessarily look for a conventional 8-hour job.

I'd try to get him into a structured routine such as:

Monday–Friday

9am–1pm: volunteering/learning

2pm–4pm: personal study or skill development

Evening: exercise, family time and limited phone/social media

And I would encourage him to learn one practical skill alongside the volunteering.

Good options for an 18-year-old include:

Computer/ICT

Graphics design

Video editing

Photography

Social media management

Digital marketing

Basic coding

CCTV installation

Phone/computer repairs

Event support

Administrative/office skills

Catering/baking

Tailoring/fashion

Automobile/mechanical skills

If he is interested in technology, for example, Bizmarrow Technologies : ICT & Computer Training school Abuja and NIIT Abuja are options for structured computer training.

One thing I particularly like about this approach

Don't tell him, "I don't want you sitting at home pressing your phone."

Tell him:

"You have a few months before university. Let's use this period to build something that will benefit you for the next five years."

That changes the conversation from punishment to opportunity.

And honestly, at 18, volunteering + learning a practical skill + preparing for university is a much better use of his time than simply staying home waiting for admission.
