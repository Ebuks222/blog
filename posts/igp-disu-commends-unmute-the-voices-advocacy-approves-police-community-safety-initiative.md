---
title: IGP Disu Commends 'Unmute the Voices' Advocacy, Approves Police Community
  Safety  Initiative.
date: 2026-07-27T00:20:00.000+01:00
---
The Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, psc(+), NPM, has commended the "Unmute the Voices Advocacy (UNVA)", a community-based campaign championed by CSP Temi Agbede-Zuokumor, in recognition of its contributions to crime prevention, child protection, and community policing.



The advocacy initiative, which began as an awareness campaign in schools, places of worship, and communities, is aimed at encouraging citizens—particularly children and young people—to speak up against abuse, crime, and other social vices while fostering stronger collaboration between the police and the public.



In a further boost to community policing efforts, the Inspector-General also approved Operation Bridge: Police Community Safety Advocacy (POCOSAAD), an initiative designed to strengthen partnerships between the Nigeria Police Force and local communities in promoting public safety and preventing crime.



Reacting to the development, CSP Agbede-Zuokumor expressed appreciation to the Inspector-General for recognising the impact of the advocacy campaign and for supporting initiatives that promote trust and cooperation between the police and members of the public.



She noted that the endorsement would further strengthen efforts to safeguard children, enhance community engagement, and encourage citizens to play active roles in crime prevention.



Reaffirming the campaign's message, she urged Nigerians to remain vigilant and report suspicious activities to security agencies, stressing that effective policing requires collective responsibility.



The advocacy continues to promote the slogan: "If You See Something, Say Something, So That We Can Do Something Together," underscoring the importance of timely information sharing in building safer communities.



The development reflects the Nigeria Police Force's growing commitment under the leadership of IGP Disu to community-oriented policing through strategic partnerships, public engagement, and proactive crime prevention initiatives.
