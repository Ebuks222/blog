---
title: IGP Disu, DEPOWA Strengthen Partnership on Welfare of Security Personnel
date: 2026-07-30T09:59:00.000+01:00
---
![](/assets/images/fc2d6047-938c-4f06-9f07-7a01161b0730_5c388b30-e88f-405a-ae7b-a79dd59f8041.jpeg)

The Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Riliwan Disu, has reaffirmed the Nigeria Police Force's commitment to improving the welfare of police personnel and their families through enhanced collaboration with the Defence and Police Officers' Wives Association (DEPOWA) and other officers' wives associations.



The IGP gave the assurance while receiving the President of DEPOWA, Mrs. Mernan Femi Oluyede, and a high-powered delegation on a courtesy visit to the Force Headquarters, Abuja.



The delegation included the President of the Police Officers' Wives Association (POWA), Chief Mrs. Mutiat Disu; President of the Nigerian Army Officers' Wives Association (NAOWA), Mrs. Safiya Shuaibu; President of the Naval Officers' Wives Association (NOWA), Mrs. Aisha Idi Abbas; President of the Nigerian Air Force Officers' Wives Association (NAFOWA), Mrs. Ngozi Aneke; and the Coordinator of the Defence Intelligence Agency Wives Association, Mrs. Jane Undiandeye.



Discussions focused on strengthening collaboration to advance the welfare of officers and their families. IGP Disu described DEPOWA as a critical partner in supporting security personnel and reaffirmed the Force's commitment to initiatives aimed at economic empowerment, psychosocial support, quality healthcare, and education to enhance the morale and operational effectiveness of personnel.



The visit further underscored the shared commitment of the Nigeria Police Force and officers' wives associations to improving the wellbeing of security personnel and their families across the country.
