*By Connect News*



*The*

 *Inspector-General of Police (IGP), Olatunji Rilwan Disu, has directed Commissioners of Police (CPs) across the country to take ownership of the media space and build stronger relationships with the public, declaring that effective communication is now an essential component of modern policing.*

*Speaking at the opening of a two-day workshop on "Enhancing Strategic Communication and Media Relations" for Commissioners of Police at the Nigeria Police Public Relations School (NPPRS), Abuja Centre, the IGP stressed that police leadership must go beyond operational excellence to include strategic public engagement.*

*According to the Force Public Relations Officer, CSP Anietie Okokon Edem Iniedu, the IGP said the office of a Commissioner of Police carries responsibilities that extend beyond its prestige, describing CPs as managers expected to provide leadership in every aspect of policing.*

*He urged the senior officers to take charge of the media narrative, emphasizing that internal security remains the core mandate of the Nigeria Police Force.*

*"Guns protect lives, but your voices protect our reputation," the IGP told the participants.*

*Disu encouraged Commissioners of Police to regard journalists as partners in nation-building rather than adversaries and advised them to cultivate healthy working relationships with the media before security challenges arise.*

*He also urged the officers to be proactive in disseminating security information, build credible public profiles, and respond promptly to emerging issues to strengthen public confidence in the Force.*

*The IGP further called on Commissioners of Police to deepen community engagement by attending social functions, visiting schools, and participating in activities that foster trust and leave lasting legacies within their respective commands.*

*He noted that strategic media engagement had not traditionally been a core part of the professional development of many officers before their appointment as Commissioners of Police, explaining that the workshop was designed to bridge that gap and equip them with the necessary communication skills.*

*Earlier in his remarks, the Provost of the Nigeria Police Public Relations School, retired Commissioner of Police Emmanuel Ojukwu, said public relations officers manage the image of the Nigeria Police Force but do not create it.*

*According to him, the true image of the Force is shaped by officers on the frontlines through their professionalism, conduct, and daily interactions with members of the public.*

*Ojukwu urged the participants to replicate the knowledge acquired during the workshop among officers serving under their various commands, noting that seven resource persons had been assembled to facilitate the training.*

*The workshop forms part of the Nigeria Police Force's ongoing efforts to strengthen strategic communication, improve police-public relations, and enhance public confidence in policing across the country.*
