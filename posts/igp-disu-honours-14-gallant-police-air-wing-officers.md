---
title: IGP Disu Honours 14 Gallant Police Air Wing Officers
date: 2026-08-06T20:53:00.000+01:00
---
In a remarkable demonstration of the Nigeria Police Force's commitment to recognising courage, sacrifice, and exceptional service, the Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, psc (+), NPM, has honoured 14 officers of the Nigeria Police Air Wing for their outstanding bravery and professionalism during dangerous anti-banditry and counter-terrorism operations across the country.

The officers, who have distinguished themselves through acts of extraordinary courage in the face of grave danger, were recognised for their unwavering commitment to duty while providing critical aerial support in some of Nigeria's most volatile security operations. Their efforts have played a vital role in intelligence gathering, surveillance, rapid deployment of personnel, casualty evacuation, logistics support, and direct operational assistance to ground troops combating banditry, terrorism, kidnapping, and other violent crimes.

Among the remarkable stories of heroism were officers who, despite sustaining gunshot wounds during operations, demonstrated exceptional composure and professionalism by continuing to pilot their aircraft to safe landing zones before seeking medical attention. Their selfless actions not only prevented the loss of valuable aviation assets but also ensured the safety of fellow crew members and personnel on board.

Their bravery exemplifies the highest ideals of policing, where service to the nation takes precedence over personal safety.

Speaking on the recognition, the Commissioner of Police in charge of the Police Air Wing, CP Ekerette Y. Udoh, commended the award recipients for their resilience, professionalism, and unwavering dedication to protecting Nigeria's territorial integrity and supporting security operations nationwide.

He noted that the Police Air Wing remains an indispensable operational arm of the Nigeria Police Force, providing strategic aerial capabilities that have significantly enhanced the Force's capacity to respond swiftly and effectively to emerging security threats across the country.

CP Udoh further expressed appreciation to the Inspector-General of Police for prioritising the welfare, motivation, and recognition of officers whose sacrifices often go unnoticed despite the enormous risks associated with aviation operations in hostile environments.

The recognition of the 14 officers reflects the leadership philosophy of IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, who has consistently emphasised merit, professionalism, courage, and dedication as core values of the Nigeria Police Force. Under his leadership, officers who demonstrate exceptional commitment to duty continue to receive commendation and institutional support, boosting morale across the Force.

For the gallant Air Wing officers, the honour is more than an award—it is a testament to the courage required to fly into dangerous territories where every mission carries significant risk. While many security operations focus on personnel on the ground, the men and women in the skies provide the critical aerial support that often determines the success of those missions.

Their recognition also serves as a reminder that behind every successful security operation are dedicated professionals who willingly place themselves in harm's way to protect lives, defend communities, and preserve national security.

As the Nigeria Police Force continues to intensify efforts against insecurity, the courage and sacrifices of these officers stand as an enduring symbol of patriotism, resilience, and selfless service.
