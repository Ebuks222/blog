---
title: IGP Disu Reaffirms Commitment to Police Reform, Backs State Policing as
  Path to Enhanced National Security.
date: 2026-07-30T10:11:00.000+01:00
---
The Inspector-General of Police (IGP), Olatunji Rilwan Disu, has reiterated the unwavering commitment of the Nigeria Police Force (NPF) to comprehensive police reforms aimed at strengthening internal security, improving public confidence, and enhancing professionalism across the Force.



The IGP made this known while addressing stakeholders at a recent security engagement, where he emphasized that reforming the Nigeria Police Force remains a top priority in line with the Federal Government's vision of building a modern, accountable, and technology-driven policing institution.



According to the Inspector-General, the ongoing reforms are focused on improving operational efficiency, intelligence gathering, community policing, officers' welfare, training, and the adoption of modern policing strategies capable of addressing emerging security threats across the country.



Speaking on the growing national conversation surrounding state policing, IGP Disu expressed support for initiatives that would strengthen grassroots security, noting that properly structured state policing could significantly improve crime prevention and rapid response to security challenges unique to different parts of the country.



He stressed that policing has evolved beyond conventional methods, requiring stronger collaboration between federal and state authorities, traditional institutions, local communities, and other critical stakeholders to effectively tackle crimes such as kidnapping, banditry, terrorism, cybercrime, armed robbery, and communal conflicts.



The police chief also reaffirmed the Nigeria Police Force's commitment to community policing, describing it as a vital strategy for rebuilding public trust and fostering stronger partnerships between the police and the communities they serve.



IGP Disu further assured Nigerians that the Force would continue investing in intelligence-led policing, digital technology, forensic investigations, and capacity building for officers to ensure more effective crime detection and prevention.



He called on citizens to continue supporting the police by providing timely and credible information capable of assisting security agencies in preventing crime and maintaining public order.



The Inspector-General also emphasized that police reform is not solely the responsibility of the Force but requires the collective support of governments at all levels, the National Assembly, civil society organizations, the private sector, and the general public.



He reaffirmed that the Nigeria Police Force remains committed to transparency, accountability, respect for human rights, and the rule of law while delivering professional policing services to all Nigerians.



The renewed commitment comes amid ongoing national discussions on constitutional reforms, security sector restructuring, and proposals for the establishment of state police as part of broader efforts to address the country's evolving security challenges.



Observers believe that sustained reforms, improved funding, enhanced training, and stronger collaboration among security stakeholders will play a crucial role in repositioning the Nigeria Police Force to effectively meet the security demands of a rapidly changing society.
