---
title: IGP DISU STORMS IMO, CHARGES OFFICERS ON PROFESSIONALISM, COMMUNITY POLICING
date: 2026-10-19T18:41:00.000+01:00
---
By Vivian Onwuasoanya



The Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, has charged officers and men of the Imo State Police Command to uphold the highest standards of professionalism, discipline and integrity, insisting that effective policing depends on public trust, intelligence gathering and stronger partnerships with communities.



Disu gave the charge during an operational visit to the Imo State Police Command Headquarters in Owerri on Thursday, September 24, 2026, as part of ongoing efforts to strengthen security operations and improve policing across the country.



The police chief, who was received by the Commissioner of Police, Imo State Command, CP Audu Garba Bosso, alongside the Assistant Inspector-General of Police, Zone 9 Headquarters, Umuahia, AIG Emeka P. Nwonyi, and other senior officers, used the visit to assess the state's security situation and review the Command's operational performance.



During the visit, the IGP received detailed briefings on ongoing crime-fighting operations, security challenges confronting the state, achievements recorded by the Command and strategies for improving operational effectiveness.



Addressing officers, stakeholders and community representatives, Disu stressed that policing would yield better results when law enforcement officers enjoy the confidence and cooperation of the people they are sworn to protect.



He urged officers to remain disciplined, professional and accountable in the discharge of their duties, while strengthening intelligence-led operations, improving relationships with residents and responding promptly to security threats.



The IGP also emphasised the need for officers to build stronger relationships with members of the public, noting that security cannot be achieved through police operations alone without the active participation of communities.



He reiterated that protecting lives and property requires a collective commitment by the Police, traditional institutions, community leaders and other stakeholders.



The visit featured an engagement with key stakeholders, including representatives of the Police Community Relations Committee (PCRC), the Nigerian Bar Association (NBA), traditional institutions, civil society organisations and community groups.



The stakeholders commended the Police for their efforts to maintain peace and security in Imo State and pledged continued support for community-based policing initiatives.



Their engagement with the IGP underscored the importance of cooperation between security agencies and citizens in tackling crime, preventing violence and building confidence in law enforcement.



As part of his engagements in the state, Disu also paid a courtesy visit to the Eze Imo, His Royal Majesty Eze Dr. E.C. Okeke, CFR.



Discussions during the visit centred on peacebuilding, conflict prevention, security collaboration and the role of traditional institutions in strengthening police-community relations.



The engagement highlighted the importance of traditional rulers, who often serve as critical links between security agencies and local communities, in promoting peaceful coexistence, resolving disputes and providing useful information to law enforcement authorities.



The operational visit forms part of the Nigeria Police Force's broader efforts to improve operational readiness, strengthen intelligence gathering and promote a policing system that places professionalism, accountability and public cooperation at its core.



With security challenges requiring both effective law enforcement and community participation, the IGP's engagements in Imo State reinforced the need for officers to combine operational efficiency with respect for citizens' rights and closer collaboration with local stakeholders.



The visit also provided an opportunity to assess the Command's needs and identify measures to improve its capacity to respond to security threats across the state.



Disu's message to officers was clear: professionalism, public trust and community cooperation must remain central to policing as the Nigeria Police Force intensifies efforts to protect lives, safeguard property and sustain peace across the country.
