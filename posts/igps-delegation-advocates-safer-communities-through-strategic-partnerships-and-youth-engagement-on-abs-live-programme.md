---
title: IGP's Delegation Advocates Safer Communities Through Strategic
  Partnerships and Youth Engagement on ABS Live Programme
date: 2026-07-30T10:14:00.000+01:00
---
The Inspector-General of Police's delegation, led by the National Coordinator of the Police Campaign Against Cultism and Other Vices (POCACOV), SP Orvenonne Ikwen, PhD, is currently participating in a live discussion on the Anambra State Broadcasting Service (ABS) on the theme, "Building a Safer Society Through Strategic Partnerships and Youth Engagement."

The programme is aimed at deepening public awareness on the importance of collective action in addressing insecurity, cultism, drug abuse, cybercrime, gender-based violence, and other social vices affecting young people and communities across Nigeria.

Speaking during the live broadcast, SP Ikwen emphasized that sustainable security cannot be achieved through law enforcement alone. He noted that meaningful collaboration among the police, government institutions, traditional rulers, religious leaders, educational institutions, civil society organizations, parents, and community members is essential to preventing crime before it occurs.

He highlighted the critical role of young people in nation-building, urging them to reject cultism, substance abuse, violent extremism, and other criminal activities capable of destroying their future. Instead, he encouraged youths to embrace education, entrepreneurship, innovation, and community service as pathways to personal development and national progress.

The Police Campaign Against Cultism and Other Vices (POCACOV), an initiative of the Nigeria Police Force, was established as a non-kinetic crime prevention strategy designed to address the root causes of crime through advocacy, public enlightenment, mentoring, and community engagement rather than relying solely on arrests and prosecution.

Through school outreach programmes, town hall meetings, media campaigns, youth empowerment initiatives, and partnerships with stakeholders, POCACOV has continued to educate young Nigerians on the dangers of cultism, drug abuse, cybercrime, trafficking, and other social vices while promoting positive values and responsible citizenship.

The initiative has become a key platform for strengthening trust between the Nigeria Police Force and the public, encouraging communities to actively participate in crime prevention and intelligence sharing.

The live discussion on ABS forms part of the Nigeria Police Force's broader commitment under the leadership of the Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, to promote preventive policing, community partnership, and youth engagement as effective tools for building safer and more resilient communities across the country.

Security stakeholders have continued to commend POCACOV for complementing conventional policing through proactive engagement, describing the initiative as a vital platform for shaping responsible youth leadership, reducing criminality, and fostering lasting peace and security in Nigeria.
