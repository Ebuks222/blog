---
title: Imo Police to Mark International Youth Day With Three-Day Engagement Programme
date: 2026-08-19T09:08:00.000+01:00
---
The Imo State Police Command, in collaboration with the State Ministry of Youth Development and Talent Hunt, is set to mark International Youth Day 2026 with a three-day Police–Youth engagement programme aimed at strengthening relations between the police and young people.

The programme, scheduled for August 20 to 22, 2026, will be held under the theme, “Different Contexts, Common Aspirations,” and will focus on responsible citizenship, crime prevention, peacebuilding and community safety.

The initiative is in line with the directive of the Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu, on deepening constructive engagement with young people and positioning them as active partners in crime prevention and community development.

The programme will begin on Thursday, August 20, with a Police–Youth Dialogue at the Senior Police Officers’ Mess, Owerri, from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Discussions will centre on cultism, drug abuse, gender-based violence, youth participation in crime prevention, community safety and responsible use of social media, including misinformation and digital thuggery.

On Friday, August 21, police officers and youths will take their engagement to the football pitch for a novelty football match at Dan Anyiam Stadium, Owerri, beginning at 3:00 p.m.

The activities will culminate on Saturday, August 22, with a Peace Walk, commencing from the Imo State Police Command headquarters at 8:00 a.m. and ending at Dan Anyiam Stadium.

The Commissioner of Police, Imo State Command, CP Audu Garba Bosso, psc(+), mnips, called on youths, youth organisations, community leaders, stakeholders and members of the public to participate actively in the programme.

He said the initiative was designed to foster mutual understanding, strengthen police-community partnerships and encourage young people to contribute meaningfully to building a safer and more peaceful Imo State.

The Command urged residents to support the initiative as part of collective efforts towards crime prevention, responsible citizenship and sustainable community development.

DSP Okoye Henry, ANIPR

Police Public Relations Officer
