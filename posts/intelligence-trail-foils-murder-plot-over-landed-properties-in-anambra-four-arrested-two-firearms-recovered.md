---
title: Intelligence Trail Foils Murder Plot Over Landed Properties in Anambra;
  Four Arrested, Two Firearms Recovered
date: 2026-08-13T13:31:00.000+01:00
---
A strategic intelligence-led operation by the Anambra State Police Command has foiled an alleged plot to murder a citizen and unlawfully take over his landed properties in Ukwulu, Dunukofia Local Government Area, with four suspects arrested and two firearms alongside live ammunition recovered.

According to the Police Public Relations Officer (PPRO), SP Ikenga Tochukwu, the breakthrough followed the renewed intelligence-driven security strategy of the Commissioner of Police, CP Nnanna Oji Ama, psc, mnips, which has strengthened the Command’s capacity to detect and disrupt criminal activities before they are carried out.

The operation was conducted on the evening of August 12, 2026, when operatives of the Rapid Response Squad (RRS), Awkuzu, working in collaboration with Agunechemba security personnel attached to Amichi, Nnewi, acted swiftly on credible intelligence concerning the alleged criminal plot.

The coordinated operation saw the security operatives move into action and arrest four suspects at different locations in Ukwulu and Amichi.

The suspects were identified as Obiora Nwankwo, 38; Chiadikobi Okoye, 36; Emmanuel Mboko, 42; and Ifeanyi Obi, 37.

In the course of the operation, security operatives also recovered two firearms and live ammunition believed to be connected to the alleged criminal activities.

Preliminary findings by investigators indicate that the suspects were allegedly involved in a conspiracy to murder a citizen and subsequently seize control of his landed properties. The Police Command, however, stressed that investigations are still ongoing to establish the full circumstances surrounding the alleged plot.

Investigators are also working to identify other individuals who may have been connected to the alleged conspiracy and determine the specific roles allegedly played by each of the four suspects.

The suspects remain in police custody and are expected to face prosecution upon the conclusion of investigations and in accordance with the law.

The development further highlights the growing emphasis on intelligence-led policing in Anambra State, as security agencies increasingly focus on identifying criminal threats before they escalate into violent attacks.

Speaking on the breakthrough, the Commissioner of Police, CP Nnanna Oji Ama, emphasized that the operation underscores the importance of proactive intelligence gathering and timely intervention in preventing crime.

According to him, the Command’s renewed intelligence-led strategy is designed to detect criminal threats early, disrupt planned attacks and protect the lives and property of residents across the state.

The successful operation also demonstrates the value of collaboration between conventional policing structures and community-based security initiatives, particularly where timely intelligence can provide security operatives with the information needed to prevent planned crimes.

As investigations continue, the Anambra State Police Command has reiterated its commitment to protecting residents and ensuring that individuals allegedly involved in violent crimes and other criminal conspiracies are brought to justice.
