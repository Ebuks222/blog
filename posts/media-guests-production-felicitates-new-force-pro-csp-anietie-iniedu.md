title: Media Guests Production Felicitates New Force PRO, CSP Anietie Iniedu
date: 2026-08-02T10:30:00.000+01:00

Media Guests Production, producers of Security Connect TV, Security Connect Magazine and Security Connect Online, heartily congratulate CSP Anietie Okokon Edem Iniedu on his well-deserved appointment as the Force Public Relations Officer (FPRO) of the Nigeria Police Force.

Your appointment is a fitting recognition of your professionalism, integrity, competence and unwavering commitment to effective policing and strategic communication. Throughout your distinguished career, you have demonstrated exceptional leadership, transparency and a remarkable ability to connect with both officers and the public.

As Head of the Complaint Response Unit, you distinguished yourself as a receptive, approachable and compassionate officer with a listening ear. Your open-door policy, accessibility and willingness to engage constructively with stakeholders earned you widespread respect and reinforced public confidence in the Nigeria Police Force.

We are confident that your wealth of operational experience, communication expertise and people-centred leadership will further strengthen police-media relations, enhance public trust and effectively project the reforms and achievements of the Nigeria Police Force under the leadership of the Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu.

As longstanding partners in promoting professionalism, accountability and positive policing through Security Connect TV, Magazine and Online, we look forward to a productive working relationship with your office.

We wish you wisdom, strength and outstanding success as you assume this important national responsibility.

Congratulations once again!

Signed:

Media Guests Production

Publishers of Security Connect TV, Security Connect Magazine & Security Connect Online
