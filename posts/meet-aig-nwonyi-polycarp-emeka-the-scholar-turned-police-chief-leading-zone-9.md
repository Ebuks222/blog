title: '"Meet AIG Nwonyi Polycarp Emeka: The Scholar-Turned Police Chief Leading
  Zone 9"'
date: 2026-08-01T20:56:00.000+01:00
---
The Nigeria Police Force has appointed AIG Nwonyi Polycarp Emeka, PhD, LLB, psc(+) as the new Assistant Inspector-General of Police in charge of Zone 9 Headquarters, Umuahia, with responsibility for policing Abia, Imo and Ebonyi States.

A highly accomplished police officer, legal practitioner and scholar, AIG Nwonyi combines decades of operational policing with an impressive academic background, holding a PhD in Applied Psychology, a Law degree, an MBA, an MPA and several other professional qualifications.

Throughout his distinguished career, he has served in several strategic positions, including Commissioner of Police in Rivers State, Force CID Annex Enugu, Commandant of the Police Staff College, Jos, and Head of Operations at the EFCC. He has also undergone specialised training in cybercrime, financial crimes, terrorism financing and intelligence in Nigeria, the United States, the United Kingdom and South Africa.

As he assumes leadership of Zone 9, AIG Nwonyi has pledged to strengthen collaboration with communities, traditional institutions, the media and other stakeholders while intensifying efforts to combat crime and enhance public safety across Abia, Imo and Ebonyi States.
