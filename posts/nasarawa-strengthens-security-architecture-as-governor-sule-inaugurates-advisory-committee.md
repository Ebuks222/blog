---
title: NASARAWA STRENGTHENS SECURITY ARCHITECTURE AS GOVERNOR SULE INAUGURATES
  ADVISORY COMMITTEE
date: 2026-08-18T22:33:00.000+01:00
---
Nasarawa State is strengthening its security architecture as Governor Abdullahi A. Sule inaugurates a high-level Security Advisory Committee to provide strategic guidance on the state's evolving security challenges.



The committee, inaugurated on Monday, August 17, 2026, is chaired by Major General Iliyasu Isah Abbah (Rtd.), with other members drawn from the retired military and police leadership.



They include AVM Bmibmitawuza David Solomon (Rtd.), DIG Mohammed Ali Ari Doma (Rtd.), Brig. Gen. Jafaru Agbashi Ebu (Rtd.), CP Isa Usman Baba (Rtd.), Wg. Cdr. Umar Mohammed Awe (Rtd.), Wg. Cdr. Mukhtar Usman (Rtd.) and CSP(C) Adamu Tanko Umar (Rtd.).



Iliya Sunday (JP), Permanent Secretary, Security and Sundry Matters, will serve as Secretary.



The committee comes at a time when Nasarawa continues to face security concerns including kidnapping, armed criminality, communal tensions and farmer-herder conflicts, particularly in vulnerable rural communities.



With the state's proximity to the Federal Capital Territory and its rapidly expanding communities, effective intelligence gathering and early response remain critical.



The committee has been mandated to advise the Governor on the day-to-day security situation, assist with intelligence gathering and provide timely and practical recommendations for maintaining public order.



Beyond responding to incidents after they occur, the new body is expected to support a more proactive and intelligence-led approach to security.



For the people of Nasarawa, the ultimate measure of success will be safer communities, improved confidence in security agencies and the ability of residents to farm, trade, travel and live without fear.



The committee has been inaugurated. The expectation now is simple: translate experience into action and make Nasarawa safer.
