---
title: NAWOJ Empowers Abuja Market Women, Promotes Health, Leadership and
  Economic Inclusion
date: 2026-07-30T15:10:00.000+01:00
---
The Nigeria Association of Women Journalists (NAWOJ) has reaffirmed its commitment to grassroots development by empowering market women in Abuja through a comprehensive advocacy outreach focused on health, leadership and economic inclusion.

The initiative, held at Wuse Market by the NAWOJ FCT Chapter in collaboration with partners, brought vital information and support directly to women traders. The programme highlighted key issues including women's health, economic empowerment, leadership development and increased political participation ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Speaking at the event, NAWOJ National Chairperson, Hajia Aisha Ibrahim, applauded the FCT chapter for taking advocacy to the grassroots. She urged women to prioritize their health, speak out against gender-based violence, embrace reproductive healthcare and actively participate in leadership and governance.

Chairperson of NAWOJ FCT, Comrade Bassey Ita Ikpang, said the outreach was designed to ensure market women have access to life-changing information and opportunities. She encouraged participants to seek help whenever necessary and remain confident in advocating for their rights.

Health experts at the event emphasized the importance of routine immunization, reproductive health awareness and enrollment in the "5 Million Women Database" initiative, which aims to improve access to healthcare services and economic opportunities.

Participants commended NAWOJ and its partners for bringing the programme directly to the marketplace, describing the initiative as timely and impactful in improving awareness of health, economic empowerment and civic engagement.

The outreach reflects NAWOJ's continued dedication to empowering women at the grassroots, ensuring they are better informed, healthier and more economically empowered to contribute meaningfully to national development.
