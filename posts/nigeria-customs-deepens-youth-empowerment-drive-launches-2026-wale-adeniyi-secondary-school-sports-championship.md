---
title: Nigeria Customs Deepens Youth Empowerment Drive, Launches 2026 Wale
  Adeniyi Secondary School Sports Championship
date: 2026-07-29T14:31:00.000+01:00
---
The Nigeria Customs Service (NCS) has reaffirmed its commitment to youth development with the official launch of the 2026 Wale Adeniyi Secondary School Sports Championship in Lagos.

The week-long competition, declared open by the Assistant Comptroller-General of Customs and Zonal Coordinator, Zone 'A', Mohammed Babandede, at the Wale Adeniyi Sports Arena, Ibafon, is aimed at discovering and nurturing young sporting talents while promoting discipline, teamwork and academic excellence.

Speaking at the event, Babandede praised the Comptroller-General of Customs, Bashir Adewale Adeniyi, for championing initiatives that empower young Nigerians through sports. He described the championship as an investment in the nation's future and a platform for identifying the next generation of sporting champions.

One of the major highlights of the championship is the Nigeria Customs Service's pledge to sponsor and mentor outstanding athletes, providing them with opportunities to pursue professional careers in sports.

The event attracted students, teachers, school proprietors, officials of the Lagos State Ministry of Education, members of NAPPS, senior Customs officers, sports enthusiasts and other stakeholders, all united in promoting grassroots sports development.

Beyond competition, the championship reflects the Nigeria Customs Service's broader commitment to youth inclusion, community engagement and national development through sports and education.
