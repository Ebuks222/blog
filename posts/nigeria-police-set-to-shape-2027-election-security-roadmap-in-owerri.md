---
title: Nigeria Police Set to Shape 2027 Election Security Roadmap in Owerri
date: 2026-08-12T21:16:00.000+01:00
---
The Nigeria Police Force is set to develop a comprehensive security roadmap for the 2027 general elections during the sixth edition of its Conference and Retreat for Senior Police Officers, scheduled to hold from September 21 to 23, 2026, in Owerri, Imo State.

The conference, themed “Developing a Nigeria Police Roadmap for Effective Management of Security During Elections,” will bring together senior police commanders, security experts, policymakers and other stakeholders to examine emerging security threats and strengthen preparations for the 2027 elections.

The Force Public Relations Officer, CSP Ani Iniedu, disclosed this during a press briefing in Abuja, explaining that the three-day event would provide a strategic platform for developing operational strategies aimed at ensuring safe, peaceful and credible elections.

According to him, discussions at the conference will focus on intelligence-led policing, police-community partnerships, digital technologies, artificial intelligence and other contemporary approaches to strengthening police operations before, during and after the elections.

He added that the retreat would facilitate the development of actionable strategies for protecting voters, electoral officials, candidates, election materials and the general public throughout the electoral process.

Iniedu said the initiative reflects the commitment of the police leadership to institutional reform, professionalisation and operational readiness ahead of the 2027 general elections.

“The Nigeria Police Force remains committed to professionalising its personnel to guarantee a peaceful, safe and secure atmosphere for the country’s next major election,” he said.

Also speaking, the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Lenders Consult International, Shina Philips, said the organisation had continued to work towards securing sponsorship for successive editions of the conference since receiving approval to organise the programme.

Philips described the initiative as part of the organisation’s contribution to supporting the “selfless and patriotic work” of the Nigeria Police Force.

He commended government efforts to strengthen national security and urged Nigerians to support the police in carrying out their constitutional responsibility of protecting lives and property and maintaining public order.

According to him, the vision of the conference has evolved over the years, attracting international participants and providing a platform for the Nigeria Police Force to embrace modern policing methods and security solutions.

Philips assured that Lenders Consult International would continue to partner with the police authorities to ensure that the conference remains focused on delivering innovative and practical solutions to contemporary policing challenges.

He also thanked the Inspector-General of Police, the Force Management Team and the entire Nigeria Police Force for their support for the programme since its inception in 2019.

He commended the Imo State Governor, Senator Hope Uzodimma, for agreeing to host the event for the third time.

The conference is expected to provide an important platform for strengthening police preparedness, improving inter-agency collaboration and developing effective strategies to address emerging security challenges ahead of the 2027 general elections.

With the elections approaching, the outcome of the retreat is expected to guide the Nigeria Police Force’s operational planning and contribute to efforts to ensure that Nigerians are able to exercise their democratic rights in a peaceful and secure environment.
