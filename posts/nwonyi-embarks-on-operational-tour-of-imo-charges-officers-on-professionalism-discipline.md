---
title: Nwonyi Embarks on Operational Tour of Imo, Charges Officers on
  Professionalism, Discipline
date: 2026-08-26T21:10:00.000+01:00
---
The Assistant Inspector-General of Police in charge of Zone 9 Police Headquarters, Umuahia, Nwonyi P. Emeka, has commenced an operational tour of Imo State to assess the security situation, review operational activities and provide strategic direction towards strengthening security across the state.



According to the Police Public Relations Officer, Imo State Command, Okoye Henry, the visit is aimed at reinforcing professional policing, effective security operations and stronger engagement with stakeholders in the state.



Nwonyi was received at the Senior Police Officers’ Mess Hall, Owerri, by the Commissioner of Police, Imo State Command, Audu Garba Bosso, alongside members of the Command’s Management Team, Area Commanders, Divisional Police Officers, Heads of Departments, Tactical Team Commanders and other officers.



Also present were members of the Police Community Relations Committee, Imo State Chapter, and the Police Officers’ Wives Association.



In his briefing, Bosso highlighted the security situation in the state, the Command’s operational achievements and challenges, while stressing the importance of intelligence-led policing, proactive deployment, inter-agency collaboration and sustained community engagement.



Addressing the officers, Nwonyi charged personnel to uphold professionalism, discipline, integrity and accountability in the discharge of their duties.



He cautioned against corruption, extortion, indiscipline, improper dressing and the unsafe handling or improper use of firearms. He also emphasised punctuality, respect for the rule of law and fundamental human rights, as well as civility towards members of the public.



Goodwill messages were delivered by the PCRC Chairman, Imo State Chapter, Evangelist Dr. Bethran Uzodinma; PCRC Chairman, Zone 9, Dr. Collins Obi; and POWA Secretary, Mrs. Princess Nnaji.



The stakeholders welcomed the visiting police chief and reaffirmed their continued support for the Police and community security efforts across Imo State.



As part of the operational tour, Nwonyi also paid a courtesy visit to the Imo State Council of Traditional Rulers, where he was received by the Chairman of the Council of Traditional Rulers and Community Policing, His Royal Majesty Eze Dr. E.C. Okeke, Eze Imo, who also serves as Chairman of the South-East Council of Traditional Rulers.



The engagement provided an opportunity to strengthen collaboration between the Police and traditional institutions in the fight against crime and other security challenges.



Nwonyi appreciated the traditional rulers for their continued support and urged stakeholders to provide security agencies with timely and actionable information that could assist in preventing and combating crime.



In response, Eze Dr. E.C. Okeke reaffirmed the commitment of the traditional institution to supporting security agencies in maintaining peace and security across the state.



The visit underscores the commitment of Zone 9 Police Headquarters and the Imo State Police Command to professional policing, effective security operations and stronger stakeholder engagement in the interest of the people of Imo State.
