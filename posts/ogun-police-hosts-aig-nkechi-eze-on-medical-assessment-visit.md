---
title: Ogun Police Hosts AIG Nkechi Eze on Medical Assessment Visit
date: 2026-08-30T19:10:00.000+01:00
---
By Security Connect



The Ogun State Police Command has hosted the pioneer Director of Force Medical Services, Assistant Inspector-General of Police, AIG Nkechi Eze, BDS, MPH, fspsp, and her team on an official working visit to the Command Headquarters, Eleweran, Abeokuta.



Received by the Commissioner of Police, CP Bode Ojajuni, the visit was more than a routine inspection. It provided an important opportunity to assess the state of police medical facilities, identify areas requiring urgent intervention and strengthen the healthcare system supporting officers who operate under demanding and often dangerous conditions.



As policing becomes increasingly complex, the health and wellbeing of police personnel remain critical to effective service delivery. Officers are frequently exposed to long working hours, operational stress, difficult environments and physical risks. Ensuring that they have access to functional and properly equipped medical facilities is therefore essential to maintaining a healthy, resilient and effective police workforce.



Speaking during the visit, AIG Nkechi Eze explained that the tour was primarily aimed at inspecting police health facilities and infrastructure across Ogun State, with a view to identifying areas requiring upgrading and additional support.



She also emphasized the importance of preventive healthcare, drawing attention to the Directorate's “Know Your Numbers” campaign. The initiative encourages personnel to regularly monitor important health indicators, including blood pressure and blood glucose levels, so that potential health challenges can be detected and addressed early.



The AIG further encouraged police officers to take advantage of compulsory health insurance schemes, noting that proactive healthcare is particularly important for personnel whose duties place considerable demands on their physical and mental wellbeing.



For the Ogun State Command, the visit also highlighted the often-overlooked role of medical personnel in police operations. Responding to the visiting team, CP Bode Ojajuni expressed appreciation to the Force Medical Services for their contribution to the Command's operational activities.



The Commissioner specifically referenced Operation Kọ̀saye, stressing the important role played by police medical personnel during field operations across the state. Their presence and support can make a significant difference when officers are injured, become ill or require immediate medical attention during demanding assignments.



Another significant point raised by the Commissioner was the opening of police medical facilities to members of the public. Beyond providing healthcare, such services can serve as an important bridge between the police and the communities they serve.



When members of the public can access quality medical services at police facilities, it creates opportunities for positive interaction, builds trust and reinforces the understanding that the police are not only responsible for law enforcement but are also partners in community wellbeing. Stronger relationships between the police and citizens ultimately contribute to more effective community policing and improved public safety.



The visit by AIG Nkechi Eze therefore carries significance beyond the inspection of buildings and medical equipment. It represents a broader effort to place the health of police personnel at the centre of operational effectiveness while strengthening the healthcare support system of the Nigeria Police Force.



A healthy officer is better positioned to respond to emergencies, protect lives, maintain public order and carry out the responsibilities of policing effectively. Investing in police healthcare is, consequently, an investment not only in the officers but also in the safety and security of the communities they serve.



The visit concluded with a guided inspection of the Command's medical facilities and a group photograph, marking an important step towards strengthening medical services and improving the welfare of police personnel in Ogun State.
