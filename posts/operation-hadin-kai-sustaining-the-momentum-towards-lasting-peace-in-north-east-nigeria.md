---
title: "Operation HADIN KAI: Sustaining the Momentum Towards Lasting Peace in
  North-East Nigeria"
date: 2026-07-29T14:25:00.000+01:00
---
For more than a decade, Nigeria's North-East has remained at the centre of the nation's fight against terrorism and insurgency. While military operations continue against remnants of terrorist groups, the focus has increasingly shifted beyond battlefield victories to creating a secure environment where communities can rebuild their lives and economic activities can flourish.

At the forefront of this mission is the Joint Task Force (North East), Operation HADIN KAI (OPHK), under the leadership of the Theatre Commander, Major General Abdulsalam Abubakar. His vision reflects a comprehensive approach to security—one that combines military operations with strategic communication, community engagement and support for post-conflict recovery.

Speaking while receiving a media delegation, Major General Abubakar reaffirmed the commitment of Operation HADIN KAI to restoring lasting peace and stability across the North-East. He noted that every military operation is carefully designed not only to neutralise terrorist threats but also to create conditions that allow displaced communities to return home, farmers to cultivate their land safely, businesses to reopen, and normal life to gradually resume.

The Theatre Commander described the media as a vital partner in the nation's security architecture. According to him, professional, balanced and objective reporting plays a significant role in shaping public understanding of military operations while countering misinformation, disinformation and extremist propaganda that often seek to undermine the morale of troops and mislead the public.

He stressed that strategic communication has become an indispensable component of modern military operations, complementing combat efforts by ensuring that accurate information reaches the public and reinforcing confidence in the Armed Forces of Nigeria.

Major General Abubakar explained that Operation HADIN KAI has continued to record significant operational successes through the integration of kinetic and non-kinetic approaches. These include intelligence-driven operations, coordinated ground and air offensives, civil-military cooperation, and sustained efforts to dismantle terrorist logistics networks that support insurgent activities.

These coordinated operations, he said, have produced measurable results across the theatre of operations. Vulnerable communities are receiving improved security, while thousands of internally displaced persons have been able to return to their ancestral homes under enhanced protection. The improved security situation has also enabled many farmers to resume agricultural activities, contributing to food production and economic recovery in the region.

The military has equally recorded the recovery of a significant cache of arms, ammunition and other combat equipment from terrorist groups. In addition, sustained pressure on insurgent enclaves has compelled several terrorist commanders, fighters and logistics collaborators to surrender, further weakening the operational capabilities of the insurgents.

Beyond the battlefield, the progress recorded by Operation HADIN KAI demonstrates the importance of a holistic approach to counter-terrorism—one that balances military strength with humanitarian considerations, strategic communication and collaboration with critical stakeholders, including the media.

As security operations continue, the Nigerian military remains optimistic that sustained cooperation among security agencies, government institutions, local communities and responsible media organisations will consolidate the gains already achieved and accelerate the restoration of lasting peace, stability and socio-economic development across Nigeria's North-East.
