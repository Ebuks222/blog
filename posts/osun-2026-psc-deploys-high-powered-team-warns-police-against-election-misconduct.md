---
title: "Osun 2026: PSC Deploys High-Powered Team, Warns Police Against Election
  Misconduct"
date: 2026-08-12T00:29:00.000+01:00
---
The Police Service Commission (PSC) has deployed a high-powered monitoring delegation to Osun State ahead of the August 15, 2026 Governorship Election to monitor the conduct of police personnel deployed for the poll.

The deployment is part of the Commission's efforts to ensure that police officers assigned to election duties adhere strictly to professional standards and the guidelines governing policing during elections.

The delegation is led by DIG (Dr) Taiwo Lakanu (rtd), Board Member and Chairman of the PSC Standing Committee on Police Matters.

The Head of Protocol and Public Relations of the Police Service Commission, Torty Njoku Kalu, ANIPR, disclosed this in a statement, noting that the delegation would monitor the conduct of police personnel before, during and after the election.

According to the Commission, particular attention would be paid to discipline, professionalism, impartiality and respect for the rights of citizens by officers deployed for the electoral exercise.

The PSC urged all police personnel assigned to Osun State to remain apolitical and ensure that eligible citizens are allowed to exercise their constitutional right to vote freely and peacefully.

The Commission stressed that police officers must conduct themselves in a manner that promotes public confidence in the electoral process and operate strictly within the provisions of the law throughout the election period.

It also urged officers to maintain professionalism in their interactions with voters, political parties, candidates, electoral officials and other stakeholders, warning against any conduct capable of compromising the credibility of the election.

The Commission reiterated its commitment to ensuring that police conduct during the election remains impartial, rule-based and civil, noting that the behaviour of security personnel is critical to maintaining public confidence in the electoral process.

The PSC further assured Nigerians that it would not hesitate to take appropriate disciplinary action against any police officer found to have compromised the integrity of the election or violated the rules guiding election duty.

The monitoring exercise is expected to provide additional oversight of police personnel across the state and reinforce the Commission's commitment to accountability and professionalism within the Nigeria Police Force.

As Osun State prepares for the August 15 governorship election, the PSC called on all police officers deployed for the exercise to discharge their duties responsibly and uphold the rights and safety of all citizens.

The Commission urged personnel to remain neutral, professional and committed to ensuring a peaceful and credible electoral process
