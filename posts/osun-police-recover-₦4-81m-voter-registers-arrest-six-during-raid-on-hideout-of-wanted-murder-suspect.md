---
title: Osun Police Recover ₦4.81m, Voter Registers, Arrest Six During Raid on
  Hideout of Wanted Murder Suspect
date: 2026-07-30T09:55:00.000+01:00
---
The Osun State Police Command has arrested six suspects and recovered ₦4,810,500, voter registers, Permanent Voter Cards (PVCs), and other exhibits during a targeted operation in the Oroki Estate area of Osogbo.

According to the Police Public Relations Officer, DSP Abiodun Ojelabi, the operation followed credible intelligence that a wanted suspect, Abiodun Arowomole, popularly known as "Ashipa," who is on the police watch list for alleged murder and other serious offences, was hiding in a residential property reportedly linked to the Secretary to the Osun State Government (SSG).

Items recovered from the premises include ₦4.81 million, a Dynabook laptop, a photocopy machine, a printer, two Permanent Voter Cards, and a voter register covering Wards 1–15.

The suspects arrested are Hon. Igbalaye Teslim (47), Akande Taiwo (60), Oladele Abiodun (38), Adeyemo Lukman (45), Olaoye Muftau (50), and Aderemi Musliu (40).

The Police said the recovery of the cash and voter register has raised suspicions of possible electoral offences, including alleged vote buying, criminal conspiracy, and harbouring a wanted suspect. The recovered exhibits are undergoing forensic examination as investigations continue.

The Command assured residents that investigations would be conducted professionally and without bias, stressing that anyone found culpable would be prosecuted in accordance with the law. It also urged members of the public to remain calm and continue supporting the Police with credible information.

The Police noted that all suspects remain presumed innocent unless proven guilty by a court of competent jurisdiction.
