---
title: Oyeniyi Unveils Tough Security Strategy, Vows Relentless Crackdown on
  Drug Cartels
date: 2026-08-01T21:18:00.000+01:00
---
The Commissioner of Police, Delta State Command, CP Olufemi Abaniwonda Oyeniyi, has reaffirmed his commitment to tackling drug trafficking and other violent crimes, unveiling a comprehensive security strategy aimed at making the state safer.

Speaking during a media briefing in Asaba, the police chief described illicit drug trafficking as a major catalyst for kidnapping, armed robbery, cultism and other violent crimes, stressing that the Command would focus on dismantling drug cartels from their roots by identifying, arresting and prosecuting the kingpins behind the illicit trade.

"We are going to tackle this drug issue from the root. We have started already and will consolidate on it to ensure drug cartels do not have their way in Delta State," Oyeniyi stated.

The Commissioner called for stronger collaboration between the Police, the Delta State Government, traditional rulers, community leaders and residents, noting that effective policing can only be achieved through collective responsibility and timely intelligence from members of the public.

He assured residents that the Command would intensify intelligence-led policing, increase police visibility across the state and strengthen crime prevention measures in line with the policing vision of the Inspector-General of Police, IGP Olatunji Rilwan Disu.

CP Oyeniyi also warned officers against misconduct, human rights abuses and unprofessional conduct, emphasizing that accountability, discipline and respect for the rule of law would remain the hallmarks of his leadership.

A seasoned law enforcement officer with vast operational and administrative experience, Oyeniyi has undergone several professional trainings in criminal investigation, intelligence-led policing and strategic leadership. He pledged to deploy his wealth of experience to enhance public safety, protect lives and property, and sustain peace across Delta State.
