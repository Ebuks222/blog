---
title: Police Arraign Eight Over Alleged ₦710m Proof-of-Funds Fraud, Recover
  Luxury Cars, Magodo Land
date: 2026-10-19T16:23:00.000+01:00
---
By Vivian Onwuasoanya



The Police Special Fraud Unit (PSFU), Lagos, has arraigned eight suspects before the Federal High Court in Lagos over an alleged ₦710 million proof-of-funds fraud, with investigations leading to the recovery of luxury vehicles and the identification of other assets suspected to be proceeds of the crime.



The suspects, Elvis Osazee Eghile, Egharevba MaryJane Osarieyekemwen, Precious Omonigho Irbhogbe, Aghatise Kelvin Uhuenkpoewen, Nweke Nnamdi Emmanuel, Obinyan Esther, Egharevba Emmanuel Osagioduwa and Emmanuel Obinyan Osenonome, were arraigned on a 12-count charge in connection with the alleged fraud.



According to a statement issued by the PSFU in Ikoyi, Lagos, the investigation began following a petition to the Commissioner of Police concerning the alleged fraudulent disbursement of ₦710 million placed on Post-No-Debit (PND) into eight designated bank accounts.



Investigations reportedly revealed that Emmanuel Obinyan Osenonome, a banker and Senior Relationship Officer, allegedly conspired with Emmanuel Egharevba to obtain the cooperation and personal identification details of other individuals to facilitate the opening of the accounts allegedly used to launder the proceeds of the crime.



The statement disclosed that forensic analysis conducted by the Corporate and Public Accounts Fraud Section of the PSFU traced significant movements of the suspected proceeds to accounts linked to individuals connected to the principal suspect.



Those identified included Nweke Nnamdi Emmanuel; Obinyan Ebehiemen Doris, a sibling of Emmanuel Obinyan; Egharevba Emmanuel Osagioduwa; and Esther Olohitare Obinyan, the wife of Emmanuel Obinyan.



Further investigations allegedly uncovered the use of forged and disputed proof-of-funds documents, indemnity documents and Post-No-Debit documents to facilitate the scheme.



As part of efforts to trace and recover assets suspected to have been acquired with the proceeds of the alleged fraud, the police identified a 2020 Lexus RX 350 valued at ₦25 million, two Toyota Camry vehicles valued at ₦8 million each, and a plot of land at Magodo Phase I, Lagos, reportedly purchased for ₦40 million.



The investigation also indicated that portions of the suspected proceeds were allegedly spent on personal financial obligations, gambling activities, CIBEX trading, the establishment and operation of a spa and beauty business, as well as furnishing the business with approximately ₦60 million.



The PSFU said the findings demonstrated the unit's capacity to trace suspected proceeds of financial crimes and identify assets allegedly acquired through fraudulent transactions.



Reacting to the development, the Commissioner of Police, PSFU, CP Eloho Edwin Okpoziakpo, reaffirmed the unit's commitment to conducting thorough, professional and evidence-based investigations, particularly in cases involving complex financial transactions and suspected abuse of banking processes.



Okpoziakpo emphasised that the unit would continue to pursue financial crimes through lawful investigative procedures, ensuring that relevant evidence is properly obtained, documented and presented before a court of competent jurisdiction.



The arraignment marks a significant development in the investigation into the alleged ₦710 million scheme, as the police continue efforts to establish the circumstances surrounding the transactions and trace assets suspected to be linked to the alleged fraud.
