---
title: Police Intensify Nationwide Operations, Recover Arms, Ammunition, Stolen Assets
date: 2026-09-14T08:59:00.000+01:00
category: "Keeping Guns Of The Streets "
---
The Nigeria Police Force has recorded fresh operational gains across seven states and the Federal Capital Territory, arresting suspected criminals and recovering firearms, 1,123 rounds of AK-47 ammunition, stolen motorcycles, rustled livestock, explosive devices and other exhibits.



The operations were carried out on September 12, 2026, by police formations and tactical teams responding to reported cases of arms trafficking, armed robbery, banditry, abduction, homicide, vandalism and attempted vehicle theft.



The Force Public Relations Officer, CSP Anietie Okokon Edem Iniedu, disclosed the operational outcomes on Monday, September 14, 2026, in the Force's latest state-by-state security update.



According to the FPRO, the operations resulted in arrests, recoveries and the rescue of a kidnapped victim, with investigations continuing across the affected commands.



Zamfara: Police Intercept Vehicle With 1,123 Rounds of Ammunition



In Zamfara State, police operatives intercepted a vehicle conveying three suspects at a checkpoint in Maru Local Government Area.



During a search of the vehicle, operatives recovered 1,123 rounds of 7.62mm AK-47 live ammunition, which were reportedly concealed inside the vehicle.



The three suspects and the recovered ammunition were subsequently taken to the State Criminal Investigation Department, Gusau, for further investigation.



Imo: Three Suspects Arrested, 13 Stolen Motorcycles Recovered



Police operatives in Imo State arrested three suspected armed robbers at a hideout and recovered 13 stolen motorcycles.



The operation also led to the recovery of an unregistered Toyota Sienna bus, motorcycle spare parts and other exhibits.



Preliminary police findings indicated that some of the motorcycles were allegedly stolen at gunpoint.



The State Criminal Investigation Department, Owerri, is investigating the case.



Katsina: Police Recover Rustled Livestock, Arrest Suspect



In Katsina State, police tactical teams engaged suspected armed bandits in a gun duel, forcing the suspects to retreat into the bush.



One suspect was arrested following the operation, while police recovered 15 cows and 35 sheep believed to have been rustled.



The recovered livestock and arrested suspect have been handed over to the SCID, Katsina, for investigation.



Katsina: Three Arrested Over Alleged Abduction of Elderly Victim



In a separate operation in Katsina, police arrested three suspects allegedly involved in the abduction of an elderly victim.



Preliminary investigation revealed that the suspects allegedly conspired to abduct the victim and collected ₦3 million ransom before releasing him after 16 days in captivity.



The suspects remain in police custody as the SCID, Katsina, continues investigation into the case.



Oyo: Kidnapped Victim Rescued Unharmed



In Oyo State, a joint team of police operatives and other security personnel rescued a kidnapped victim following a coordinated search of surrounding bushes in pursuit of the abductors.



The sustained pressure reportedly forced the kidnappers to abandon the victim and flee.



The victim was rescued unharmed, while the SCID, Ibadan, continues investigation aimed at identifying and apprehending the fleeing suspects.



Gombe: Police Recover Stolen Motorcycle, Explosive Devices



Gombe State recorded two separate operational successes during the reporting period.



In the first incident, police arrested a suspected armed robber following an attack on a residence and recovered a stolen motorcycle, two locally made explosive devices and other exhibits reportedly abandoned by fleeing suspects.



In a separate operation, another suspected armed robbery suspect was arrested following an attack on a residence, while a second stolen motorcycle was recovered.



The two cases are being investigated by the SCID, Gombe.



Delta: Suspect Arrested With Firearm, 18 Live Cartridges



In Delta State, police operatives arrested a suspect allegedly found in possession of a pump-action firearm and 18 live cartridges.



The suspect and recovered exhibits have been transferred to the SCID, Asaba, for investigation.



FCT: Police Arrest Suspects Over Homicide, Vandalism and Attempted Car Theft



In the Federal Capital Territory, police operatives recorded three separate arrests involving alleged homicide, vandalism and attempted vehicle theft.



In the homicide case, operatives traced and arrested a suspect allegedly linked to the stabbing death of a male victim. The suspect had reportedly fled the scene after the incident.



The CID, Abuja, is investigating the case.



In a separate operation, police operatives, working with local vigilantes, arrested a suspect allegedly involved in the vandalisation of infrastructure. Stolen armoured cables were recovered alongside a saw and pliers reportedly used in the operation.



Police also arrested another suspect at the National Stadium following a distress call over an alleged attempt to steal a Toyota Sienna vehicle. The suspect was apprehended at the scene and the case is being investigated by the SCID, Abuja.
