---
title: Police Recall Retired AIG Igwe, Owohunwa, 473 Others, Order Mandatory
  Six-Month Induction
date: 2026-08-03T10:33:00.000+01:00
---
In a significant move aimed at strengthening the operational capacity of the Nigeria Police Force, the Inspector-General of Police has approved the recall of retired senior police officers, including former Assistant Inspectors-General of Police (AIGs) Kanayo Uzuegbu Igwe and Stephen Hasso Owohunwa, alongside 473 other retired officers.



As part of the recall programme, all the affected officers will undergo a mandatory six-month induction course designed to update their knowledge of contemporary policing, emerging security threats, intelligence-led operations, modern investigative techniques, and evolving legal and human rights standards.



The induction programme is expected to equip the recalled officers with the skills required to adapt to the changing dynamics of policing and national security. It will also provide an opportunity to familiarise them with recent reforms, technological innovations, and operational strategies introduced within the Nigeria Police Force.



Security experts have noted that the decision to recall experienced retired officers reflects an effort to leverage institutional memory and professional expertise in addressing Nigeria's evolving security challenges. With decades of policing experience, many of the recalled officers are expected to contribute to mentorship, capacity building, strategic planning, investigations, intelligence gathering, and specialised operational assignments.



The move comes at a time when the Nigeria Police Force continues to implement reforms aimed at improving service delivery, enhancing professionalism, and strengthening its ability to respond effectively to emerging criminal threats across the country.



The mandatory induction underscores the Force's recognition that policing has evolved considerably, making continuous training essential even for experienced officers returning to service. The programme is expected to reinforce professionalism, ethical conduct, respect for human rights, and adherence to current operational procedures.



Observers believe that the combination of experienced leadership and modern policing techniques could further enhance the Force's operational effectiveness, particularly in intelligence-driven policing, crime prevention, and strategic security management.



The recall initiative represents another step in the Nigeria Police Force's broader efforts to strengthen institutional capacity and improve public safety through the deployment of experienced personnel alongside ongoing reforms.
