---
title: Police Record Arrests, Rescues, Weapons Recovery as Operations Intensify
  Nationwide
date: 2026-08-17T14:04:00.000+01:00
---


The Nigeria Police Force has recorded a series of operational successes across several states, including arrests of suspects linked to kidnapping and murder, recovery of firearms, rescue of kidnapped victims and ongoing investigations into violent crimes.

The development was contained in the latest 48-hour operational situation report released by the Force Public Relations Officer.

In Niger State, Police operatives recovered the bodies of five persons following a canoe accident on the Kudi River in Katcha Local Government Area. The victims were buried according to Islamic rites, while the State Criminal Investigation Department, Minna, continues investigations.

In Taraba State, two suspects were arrested in Maraba Kunini, Lau LGA, over an alleged kidnapping and murder case. Police said preliminary interrogation linked the suspects to the abduction of two persons and the fatal shooting of two others at Jimlari Village.

In Imo State, Police operatives, working with local vigilantes, rescued two kidnapped victims from a forest. Two suspected kidnappers were reportedly neutralised during a gun battle, while others escaped with gunshot injuries. Sadly, one vigilante was fatally wounded during the operation.

Kano State also recorded a major response after Police operatives were alerted to a shooting at a wedding ceremony in Tudun Yola Bajallabe. A suspect was arrested and the firearm allegedly used in the incident recovered. Four victims were evacuated for medical treatment, although one was confirmed dead.

In Osun State, detectives commenced investigation after the body of an unidentified woman was discovered inside a church premises in Obelewo, Osogbo. Police said preliminary findings indicated that the victim had been killed and her left wrist severed. The corpse was deposited at a mortuary for autopsy.

Meanwhile, in the Federal Capital Territory, three suspects were arrested following the death of a security guard after a reported altercation with neighbours in Dawaki. The CID Abuja is investigating the circumstances surrounding the incident.

In Bauchi State, Police patrol teams, local hunters and vigilantes responded to a suspected bandit attack in Sabon Jiri Village, Duguri District. One person was killed while another was abducted. Security operations are ongoing to rescue the victim and apprehend the attackers.

In Adamawa State, Police also arrested a prime suspect following a violent incident at a hotel in Girei Local Government Area and recovered a weapon in connection with the case.

The series of operations highlights the increasingly intelligence-driven approach being adopted by the Nigeria Police Force, with rapid responses to distress calls, intelligence gathering, collaboration with community-based security structures and targeted investigations forming part of the ongoing crime-fighting strategy.

While several investigations remain ongoing, the developments demonstrate the importance of timely information from members of the public and effective coordination between the Police, local vigilantes and other security stakeholders.

The Nigeria Police Force continues to urge citizens to remain vigilant and provide credible information that can assist security agencies in preventing crime, rescuing victims and bringing suspected offenders to justice.
