AIG Agbonika officially assumed duty following his deployment by the Nigeria Police Force, succeeding AIG Salman-Dogo Garba, PSC, MNIPS, who recently retired from active service.

A seasoned police officer with over three decades of distinguished service, AIG Agbonika has built an outstanding career in criminal investigation, operations, administration, and strategic leadership. Prior to his promotion to the rank of Assistant Inspector-General of Police, he served meritoriously as the Commissioner of Police, Edo State Command, where he demonstrated exemplary leadership and professionalism.

Media Guests Productions is confident that his wealth of experience, academic excellence, and commitment to policing will further strengthen security, enhance public confidence, and promote effective policing across Edo and Delta States.

We wish AIG Monday Agbonika a successful, impactful, and fulfilling tenure as he takes on this important responsibility in the service of the nation.
