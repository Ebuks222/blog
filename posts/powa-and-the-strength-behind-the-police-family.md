lice Officers’ Wives Association (POWA), Abia State Command, has once again demonstrated that the strength of policing extends beyond the men and women in uniform to the families who stand firmly behind them.



On Monday, September 28, 2026, the Abia State POWA Executive, led by its Chairperson, Mrs. Abigail Afolabi, paid a courtesy visit to the Commissioner of Police, Abia State Command, CP Wilfred Olutokunbo Afolabi, psc(+), mnips, JP, at the Command Headquarters.



The visit was more than a traditional courtesy call. It provided an opportunity to strengthen the relationship between the Command leadership and POWA while drawing attention to the important role officers’ wives play in the welfare, stability and emotional wellbeing of police families.



Speaking during the visit, Mrs. Abigail Afolabi appreciated the Commissioner of Police for his leadership and support for police officers and their families. She reaffirmed the association’s commitment to programmes aimed at promoting family welfare, education, emotional intelligence, healthcare and community development.



These areas remain particularly important in a profession where officers are frequently confronted with demanding operational responsibilities, long hours away from home and the pressures that come with maintaining public safety.



POWA, therefore, serves as an important bridge between the police institution and the family. Its activities provide an avenue for officers’ wives to support one another, promote family wellbeing and contribute meaningfully to the broader objectives of the Nigeria Police Force.



Responding, CP Wilfred Olutokunbo Afolabi appreciated the association for the visit and commended POWA for its continuous support and commitment to the welfare of serving police officers.



The Commissioner also highlighted the critical role of officers’ wives on the home front, urging them to continue supporting their husbands while giving proper attention to the care, upbringing and development of their children.



His message underscores an important reality: effective policing does not begin and end at the police station. Behind every officer responding to an emergency, maintaining law and order or spending long hours on duty is often a family making sacrifices and providing the emotional foundation that enables that officer to serve.



The Commissioner of Police consequently assured the new POWA executive of the Command’s full support in achieving its mandate and implementing programmes designed to improve the welfare of police families.



For Security Connect Media, the Abia POWA visit is a reminder that police reform and institutional welfare must also recognise the family as an important component of policing.



A motivated officer needs a stable support system. A stable police family contributes to the emotional strength of the officer, while a healthy relationship between Command leadership and officers’ families can further strengthen morale and institutional unity.



As the new POWA leadership begins its work in Abia State, its success will not only be measured by the programmes it organises, but by the positive impact those programmes have on police families and, ultimately, on the officers who serve the people.



The police family remains an often-unseen pillar of public safety. Supporting that pillar is, in many ways, supporting the men and women who wear the uniform.
