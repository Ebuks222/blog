---
title: POWA President Commissions Projects, Reviews Passing-Out Parade at Police
  Secondary School, Minna
date: 2026-07-26T11:13:00.000+01:00
---
![](/assets/images/091a151a-50bd-4893-8e00-5f35b365136e.jpeg)

The National President of the Police Officers' Wives Association (POWA) and wife of the Inspector-General of Police, Chief Mrs. Mutiat Olufunmilola Disu, has reaffirmed her commitment to the welfare of police personnel and their families during an official visit to the Niger State Police Command.

A major highlight of the visit was her role as the Reviewing Officer at the 29th Passing-Out Parade of the Police Secondary School, Minna, where 420 students graduated for the 2025/2026 academic session.

In furtherance of POWA's welfare and development agenda, Chief Mrs. Disu commissioned key infrastructure projects within the school, including a newly constructed Guest House and Staff Quarters. She also performed the foundation-laying ceremony for the proposed POWA Quarters, describing the projects as part of ongoing efforts to improve educational facilities and the living conditions of police personnel and their families.

The POWA President also paid a courtesy visit to the Emir of Minna, His Royal Highness, Alhaji (Dr.) Umaru Farouq Bahago, CON, where she expressed appreciation for the traditional ruler's continued support for security, peace, and community development in Niger State.

In recognition of her contributions and to commemorate her historic visit, the Emir conferred on her the traditional title of "Jauharan Minna", meaning "The Precious Daughter of Minna."

The event attracted several senior police officers, government officials, and POWA executives, including the Commissioner of Police, Niger State Command, CP Adamu Abdullahi Elleman, mnips, accompanied by his wife and Chairperson of POWA, Niger State Command, Mrs. Rakiya Adamu Elleman. Also present were the wife of the AIG, Zone 7, Evang. Angela Olaiya; the Sector Commander of the Federal Road Safety Corps, CC Aishat Sa'adu; the Force Education Officer, DCP Ibidapo Oludare Olubiyi; and other senior officers.

The Governor of Niger State was represented by his Principal Private Secretary, Hon. Emmanuel Umar, who conveyed the governor's goodwill message and announced the state government's support and donation towards the continued growth and development of the Police Secondary School, Minna.

The visit underscored POWA's sustained commitment to educational development, officers' welfare, and stronger collaboration with traditional institutions and government stakeholders in advancing the Nigeria Police Force's objectives.
