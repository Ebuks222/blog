title: PSC Appoints AIG Auwal as POLAC Commandant, Deploys Eight Commissioners
  of Police to State Commands By Security Connect

---
The Police Service Commission (PSC) has approved the posting of Assistant Inspector-General of Police (AIG) Auwal Musa Mohammed as the new Commandant of the Nigeria Police Academy (POLAC), Wudil, Kano State, alongside the deployment of eight Commissioners of Police to various state commands across the country.

The development was disclosed in a statement issued by the Commission's Head of Protocol and Public Relations, Mr. Torty Njoku Kalu, ANIPR, on Wednesday, August 5, 2026.

According to the statement, the approvals were made during a Commission meeting presided over by Hon. Justice Paul Adamu Galumje (JSC, rtd), Board Member and Honourable Commissioner I.

AIG Auwal is a highly experienced police officer with over 33 years of service in operations, investigations, intelligence-led policing, and police training. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Political Science and a Master's degree in Public Policy from Bayero University, Kano.

During his distinguished career, he has served as Commandant of the Police College, Maiduguri; Commissioner of Police, Bauchi State Command; and Assistant Inspector-General of Police in Zones 3 and 6. He is also a member of the International Association of Chiefs of Police.

The Commission also approved the deployment of eight Commissioners of Police to state commands to fill vacancies created by recent promotions and enhance operational effectiveness. The affected officers are CP Afolabi Wilfred Olutokunbo (Abia), CP Ayodeji Faniyan, fdc (Plateau), CP Saka Adewale Ajao (Bayelsa), CP Ajo Geoffrey Ordue (Cross River), CP Nnanna Oji Ama (Anambra), CP Patrick Daaor (Edo), CP Tijani Murtala (Jigawa), and CP Samuel Erale Etaifo (Delta).

Speaking during the meeting, Hon. Justice Galumje charged the newly posted senior officers to remain steadfast, uphold the rule of law, and discharge their responsibilities with neutrality, professionalism, and diligence.

Other members of the Commission in attendance included Alhaji Abdulfatah Mohammed, Hon. Justice Christine Ladi Dabup (rtd), and the Secretary to the Commission, Chief Onyemuche Nnamani.

The postings are part of the Commission's ongoing efforts to strengthen leadership within the Nigeria Police Force and enhance effective policing, accountability, and service delivery across the country.
