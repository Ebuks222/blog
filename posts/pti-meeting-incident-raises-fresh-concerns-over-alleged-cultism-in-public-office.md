The recent altercation following the APC meeting at the Petroleum Training Institute (PTI) has sparked concerns beyond politics, with reports suggesting the incident may have stemmed from an alleged clash between rival cult groups rather than political differences.

Although the claims remain unverified, the incident has renewed calls for security agencies to conduct a thorough and impartial investigation to establish the facts and bring anyone found culpable to justice.

The development also highlights the need for public office holders to exercise caution in the choice of associates. Public confidence in government depends not only on leadership but also on the integrity and conduct of those entrusted with official responsibilities.

As Nigeria continues to grapple with insecurity, political leaders and public institutions must remain vigilant against any association with individuals linked to criminal activities. Upholding transparency, accountability and professionalism remains essential to strengthening public trust and safeguarding democratic institutions.
