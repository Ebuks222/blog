---
title: PTML Customs Leads the Way in Digital Cargo Management with Electronic
  Cargo Tracking System
date: 2026-08-02T13:08:00.000+01:00
---
In a significant step towards modernising cargo management and strengthening trade facilitation in Nigeria, the Port & Terminal Multiservices Limited (PTML) Area Command of the Nigeria Customs Service (NCS) has commenced stakeholder sensitisation ahead of the full implementation of the Electronic Cargo Tracking System (ECTS).

The initiative, championed by the Comptroller-General of Customs, Dr. Bashir Adewale Adeniyi, reflects the Service's unwavering commitment to building a transparent, technology-driven, and globally competitive 21st-century Customs administration.

Speaking during a stakeholders' workshop held at the PTML Conference Hall, the Acting Customs Area Controller, Deputy Comptroller Nura Miko, disclosed that PTML was selected as the second pilot command for the nationwide rollout of the ECTS after the successful deployment of the system at the Apapa Area Command. The selection underscores PTML's strategic importance in facilitating trade, improving operational efficiency, and contributing significantly to national revenue generation.

The Electronic Cargo Tracking System marks a departure from the traditional reliance on physical Customs escorts for cargo moving under transire to bonded terminals, other Customs commands, and Free Trade Zones. By leveraging real-time GPS technology, the system will monitor cargo movement from departure to final destination, instantly detect route deviations or seal tampering, enhance cargo security, improve manpower utilisation, and strengthen revenue assurance.

Beyond improving Customs operations, the ECTS promises immense benefits for the trading community. Faster cargo clearance, reduced demurrage costs, lower logistics expenses, enhanced transparency, and improved predictability in cargo movement will significantly ease the cost of doing business while boosting stakeholders' confidence in Nigeria's trade environment.

The sensitisation workshop provided Customs officers, licensed customs agents, freight forwarders, terminal operators, and other stakeholders with a clear understanding of their roles, responsibilities, and expectations under the new system, ensuring readiness for its successful implementation.

Participants praised the visionary leadership of the Comptroller-General of Customs and the management of the Nigeria Customs Service for driving reforms that align the Service with global best practices. Their commendation reflects growing confidence that digital innovations such as the Electronic Cargo Tracking System will further enhance efficiency, accountability, cargo security, and trade competitiveness in Nigeria.

As the Nigeria Customs Service continues to embrace innovation and digital transformation, the implementation of the Electronic Cargo Tracking System at PTML represents another milestone in its journey towards delivering world-class Customs services that support economic growth, facilitate legitimate trade, and strengthen national development.
