---
title: Retired DSP Defends Nigeria Police Against Corruption Allegations
date: 2026-07-30T15:00:00.000+01:00
---
A retired Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP), Chief Anyanwu, has defended the Nigeria Police Force, arguing that it is unfairly labelled as Nigeria's most corrupt institution while similar practices exist across other public agencies.

Speaking on his 35 years of service, Anyanwu said roadblocks, bail procedures and the exchange of gifts are not exclusive to the police, noting that other security agencies and government institutions carry out similar responsibilities.

While acknowledging that gratification is against the law, he alleged that many senior public officials receive festive gifts from influential individuals, yet public criticism remains largely focused on the police.

He, however, praised former Commissioner of Police Alhaji Bukar Ali for his integrity, describing him as an officer who consistently rejected gifts and inducements throughout his career.

The retired officer urged serving policemen not to lose hope despite public criticism, adding that everyone would ultimately be judged by God. He concluded by quoting the Bible: "All have sinned and come short of the glory of God."
