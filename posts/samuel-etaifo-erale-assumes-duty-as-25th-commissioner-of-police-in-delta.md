---
title: Samuel Etaifo Erale Assumes Duty as 25th Commissioner of Police in Delta
date: 2026-09-01T23:54:00.000+01:00
---
By Vivian Onwuasoanya



The Delta State Police Command has welcomed a seasoned police officer and administrator, Samuel Etaifo Erale,  as the 25th Commissioner of Police in the state, bringing to the command an impressive career spanning three decades of operational policing, administration, investigation, peacekeeping and leadership.



Erale takes over from Yemi John Oyeniyi,and assumes responsibility for leading the Delta State Police Command at a time when effective crime prevention, intelligence-led policing and stronger community partnerships remain critical to the security of the state.



Born on 2 March 1968 at Pedro Police Barracks, Shomolu, Mainland, Lagos State, Erale hails from Owan East Local Government Area of Edo State.



He obtained a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc. Hons) degree in Political Science from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, in 1992, before joining the Nigeria Police Force in 1994 as a member of Course 18 Cadet Assistant Superintendents of Police.



Since his enlistment, Erale has built a diverse policing career, serving in various commands and formations across Nigeria. His career began in Kogi State Command, after which he served in Ekiti, Osun, Akwa Ibom, Bayelsa and Rivers State Commands, as well as Zone 7 Headquarters and Force Headquarters, Abuja.



His professional experience extends beyond Nigeria. In 2000, he served as part of the Nigeria Police Force contingent to the United Nations Mission in Kosovo, gaining valuable international peacekeeping experience.



One of the notable aspects of his career was his service in Osun State, where he played a key role in election security and was involved in the conduct of elections in the state. The assignment provided him with additional experience in managing sensitive security operations, coordinating personnel and maintaining peace during electoral activities.



His previous appointments include Personal Assistant to the Commissioner of Police, Kogi State Command; Personal Assistant to the Assistant Inspector-General of Police, Zone 7, Abuja; Provost, Police Mobile Force; Squadron Commander, MOPOL 33, Ekiti State, from 2008 to 2013; and Chief Superintendent of Police, Administration, Osun State Command.



At the Office of the Force Secretary, Force Headquarters, Abuja, Erale served in the Promotions Section at different stages of his career, including as Chief Superintendent of Police and later Assistant Commissioner of Police.



His years in the Promotions Section exposed him extensively to police administration, personnel management, promotions and career development. He also served at the Special Investigation Unit of the Force Criminal Investigation Department, Abuja, further strengthening his investigative background.



With experience cutting across operations, investigations, administration, personnel management, international peacekeeping and command, Erale brings considerable institutional knowledge to his new assignment in Delta State.



His assumption of duty is expected to further strengthen the command's efforts against crime and criminality while deepening collaboration between the police and communities across the state.



The Delta State Police Command has called on residents, traditional rulers, community leaders, stakeholders and other well-meaning members of the public to extend their support and cooperation to the new Commissioner as he begins his tenure.



As he takes the reins of the Delta Command, all eyes will be on Erale to translate his wealth of experience into effective policing, improved public safety and stronger confidence between the police and the people.



For a police officer whose career has taken him through diverse operational environments, specialised formations, international peacekeeping and sensitive electoral assignments, his new role in Delta represents another significant chapter in a long and distinguished policing career.
