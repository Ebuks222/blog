title: Security Connect Celebrates Nigerian Army’s First Female Spokesperson,
  Colonel Onyinyechi Anele, on Her Promotion
date: 2026-08-14T12:28:00.000+01:00
---
The beautiful moment Nigerian Army’s first female spokesperson, Colonel Onyinyechi Apollonia Anele, was promoted from the rank of Lieutenant Colonel to Colonel on December 9, 2025, remains a remarkable milestone worthy of celebration.

Her promotion is a testament to her dedication, professionalism, discipline and distinguished service to the Nigerian Army. As the first female spokesperson of the Nigerian Army, Colonel Anele has continued to break barriers and inspire women through her exemplary career and commitment to national service.

On behalf of Security Connect TV & Magazine, I warmly congratulate Colonel Onyinyechi Apollonia Anele on this well-deserved promotion. We celebrate your achievement and wish you greater wisdom, strength and success as you continue to serve the nation with honour and distinction.

Congratulations, Ma. May you continue to soar to greater heights. God bless you, the Nigerian Army and the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
