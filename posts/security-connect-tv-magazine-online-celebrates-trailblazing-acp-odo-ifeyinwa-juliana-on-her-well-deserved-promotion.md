title: Security Connect TV, Magazine & Online Celebrates Trailblazing ACP Odo
  Ifeyinwa Juliana on Her Well-Deserved Promotion
date: 2026-07-30T09:53:00.000+01:00
---
The Publisher, Management, Editorial Board, and Staff of Security Connect TV, Magazine & Online heartily congratulate Assistant Commissioner of Police (ACP) Odo Ifeyinwa Juliana on her well-deserved promotion from the rank of Chief Superintendent of Police (CSP) to Assistant Commissioner of Police (ACP).

Your elevation is a fitting recognition of your unwavering professionalism, integrity, dedication, and exceptional service to the Nigeria Police Force and the nation at large.

ACP Odo Ifeyinwa Juliana made history as the first female Area Commander of Aba Area Command in Abia State, where she distinguished herself through exemplary leadership, effective crime prevention strategies, and strong community policing initiatives. Her achievements have continued to inspire women in policing and have further demonstrated that excellence in leadership knows no gender.

Throughout your distinguished career, you have remained committed to protecting lives and property, upholding justice, and promoting professionalism within the Force. Your promotion is a reflection of your hard work, resilience, and invaluable contributions to national security.

As you assume your new responsibilities as an Assistant Commissioner of Police, we pray that God grants you greater wisdom, strength, and divine guidance to excel in this higher office. May your tenure be marked by remarkable achievements and continued success in advancing the ideals of effective policing and public service.

On behalf of Security Connect TV, Magazine & Online, we celebrate this important milestone and wish you many more years of impactful service, outstanding accomplishments, and well-deserved promotions.

Congratulations once again, ACP Odo Ifeyinwa Juliana. We wish you continued success and excellence in your distinguished career.
