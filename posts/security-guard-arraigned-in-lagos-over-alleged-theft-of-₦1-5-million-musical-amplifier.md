---
title: Security Guard Arraigned in Lagos Over Alleged Theft of ₦1.5 Million
  Musical Amplifier
date: 2026-07-30T09:57:00.000+01:00
---
> A 24-year-old security guard, Joseph Nwakolo, has been arraigned before an Ikeja Magistrate Court sitting in Ogba, Lagos State, over the alleged theft of a musical amplifier valued at ₦1.5 million.
>
>
>
> Nwakolo was brought before the court on charges bordering on conspiracy and stealing, following an investigation by officers attached to the Area "G" Command, Ogba.
>
>
>
> According to the charge sheet, the alleged offence occurred on May 18, 2026, at about 9:30 a.m., at a music office located at No. 2 Coker Street, Off College Road, Ogba, within the Ikeja Magisterial District.
>
>
>
> The Commissioner of Police, Lagos State Command, is the complainant in the case, while the defendant was charged alongside other suspects who are currently at large.
>
>
>
> The prosecution alleged that Nwakolo, who was employed as the security guard responsible for the premises, conspired with the fleeing suspects to steal a Seer Amplifier valued at ₦1,500,000, belonging to Osundande Ayodeji.
>
>
>
> The alleged offences are said to contravene Section 411 of the Criminal Law of Lagos State, 2015, relating to conspiracy to commit a felony, and Section 280(7) of the same law, which prescribes punishment for the offence of stealing.
>
>
>
> During the arraignment, the defendant pleaded not guilty to both charges.
>
>
>
> The prosecuting counsel, SP Josephine Ihkayere, urged the court to fix a trial date to enable the prosecution present evidence in support of the allegations.
>
>
>
> In his ruling, the presiding Magistrate, Mr. L.A. Owolabi, granted the defendant bail with two sureties, who must provide evidence of their means of livelihood and tax payment. Pending the fulfilment of the bail conditions, the court ordered that the defendant be remanded at the Kirikiri Correctional Centre.
>
>
>
> Court documents indicate that the matter is being investigated by Inspector Omegwu Sunday of the Area "G" Command.
>
>
>
> The owner of the amplifier, Osundande Ayodeji, is expected to testify during the trial. The amplifier is reportedly used for musical performances and studio productions.
>
>
>
> Reacting to the incident, security consultants noted that cases of alleged insider theft involving security personnel have become a growing concern for many businesses. They advised employers to strengthen internal security measures by installing CCTV surveillance systems, carrying out thorough background checks on employees, and implementing stricter access control procedures.
>
>
>
> The case has been adjourned for further proceedings.
>
>
>
> The defendant is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty by a court of competent jurisdiction.
