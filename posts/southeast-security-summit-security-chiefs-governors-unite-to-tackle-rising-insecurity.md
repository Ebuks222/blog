---
title: "Southeast Security Summit: Security Chiefs, Governors Unite to Tackle
  Rising Insecurity"
date: 2026-07-30T22:19:00.000+01:00
---
With insecurity remaining one of the biggest challenges confronting Nigeria's Southeast, key security stakeholders have reaffirmed their commitment to adopting a united and intelligence-driven approach to restoring peace and stability across the region.

This formed the focus of the Southeast Security Summit held on Thursday, July 30, 2026, in Umuahia, where the Commissioner of Police, Enugu State Command, CP Mamman Bitrus Giwa, psc, mspsp, smicm, represented the Assistant Inspector-General of Police in charge of Zone 13, AIG Miller Gajere Dantawaye, psc.

CP Giwa joined the Assistant Inspector-General of Police in charge of Zone 9, AIG Nwonyi Polycarp Emeka, PhD, alongside governors of the Southeast states, senior federal and state government officials, heads of security agencies, traditional rulers, and other critical stakeholders at the summit.

Organised by the Federal Ministry of Defence under the leadership of the Honourable Minister of Defence, General Christopher G. Musa (Rtd), and hosted by the Governor of Abia State, the summit was themed "Collaborative Approaches to Strengthening Security and Regional Stability."

The meeting comes against the backdrop of persistent security concerns in the Southeast, where incidents of kidnapping, attacks on security personnel and public facilities, armed criminality, separatist-related violence, illegal sit-at-home enforcement, and communal conflicts have continued to threaten lives, livelihoods, and economic activities.

Stakeholders noted that while security agencies have recorded operational successes, the evolving nature of these threats requires stronger collaboration among federal and state governments, security agencies, traditional institutions, community leaders, and citizens.

Discussions centred on intelligence sharing, improved inter-agency cooperation, community engagement, technology-driven policing, and proactive measures aimed at addressing the root causes of insecurity while strengthening public confidence in security institutions.

The participation of senior police leadership, including CP Giwa on behalf of AIG Dantawaye, underscores the Nigeria Police Force's continued commitment to partnering with relevant stakeholders to enhance security, safeguard lives and property, and promote sustainable peace across the Southeast.

As the summit concluded, participants expressed optimism that sustained collaboration and coordinated action would significantly improve the region's security architecture, encourage economic growth, and reinforce public confidence in ongoing efforts to restore lasting peace.
