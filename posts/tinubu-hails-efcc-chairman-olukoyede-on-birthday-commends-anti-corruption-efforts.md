---
title: Tinubu Hails EFCC Chairman Olukoyede on Birthday, Commends
  Anti-Corruption Efforts
date: 2026-10-17T18:37:00.000+01:00
---
By Vivian Onwuasoanya 



President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has congratulated the Executive Chairman of the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC), Mr Olanipekun Olukoyede, on his birthday, commending his leadership in the fight against economic and financial crimes and the promotion of accountability in Nigeria.



In a birthday message issued by his Special Adviser on Information and Strategy, Bayo Onanuga, President Tinubu acknowledged Olukoyede’s role in steering the anti-graft agency’s efforts to combat financial crimes, strengthen institutional accountability and advance transparency in public service.



The President noted that the EFCC remains a critical institution in Nigeria’s pursuit of good governance, stressing the importance of safeguarding public resources, upholding the rule of law and ensuring that integrity remains central to national development.



Tinubu also reaffirmed the Federal Government’s commitment to building a nation where accountability, respect for the law and responsible management of public resources are upheld as essential pillars of governance.



The President’s message further highlighted the milestones and achievements recorded by the EFCC since Olukoyede assumed office in 2023, expressing optimism that the Commission would continue to make meaningful contributions to the nation’s economic and institutional development.



He expressed hope that the new year in Olukoyede’s life would usher in greater opportunities for service, renewed accomplishments and sustained progress in the collective pursuit of a just, transparent and prosperous Nigeria.



President Tinubu joined Olukoyede’s family, colleagues, staff of the Commission and well-wishers in celebrating the occasion, wishing the EFCC chairman many more years of fulfilment, good health and dedicated service to the Federal Republic of Nigeria.



The congratulatory message underscores the importance the Federal Government attaches to the EFCC’s mandate and its role in promoting financial integrity, strengthening public confidence in institutions and supporting the fight against corruption.



As Olukoyede marks another year, the President’s goodwill message places renewed emphasis on the need for sustained efforts to combat economic and financial crimes and entrench accountability in the country’s public and private sectors.
