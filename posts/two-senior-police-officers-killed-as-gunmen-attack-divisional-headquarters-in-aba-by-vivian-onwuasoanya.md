---
title: Two Senior Police Officers Killed as Gunmen Attack Divisional
  Headquarters in Aba  By Vivian Onwuasoanya
date: 2026-10-17T18:30:00.000+01:00
---
Two senior police officers have been killed, while three Inspectors sustained injuries following an attack by unidentified armed hoodlums on a divisional police headquarters in the Urratta-Eziukwu area of Aba, Abia State, in the early hours of Saturday, October 17, 2026.



The attack, which occurred at about 2:26 a.m., involved the use of explosives that caused a fire, damaging parts of the police facility and a divisional patrol van.



The Abia State Police Command disclosed this in a press release, confirming that the injured officers were receiving medical attention. The Command also clarified that no firearms or ammunition were carted away by the attackers during the incident.



Following the attack, the Commissioner of Police, Abia State Command, CP Wilfred Olutokunbo Afolabi, personally led an operational team comprising tactical units to the affected area.



According to the Command, the team combed the surrounding environment and restored calm, while intensified security operations and surveillance have been launched to track down and apprehend those responsible for the attack.



CP Afolabi condemned the incident, describing it as an unacceptable act of violence against law enforcement officers and assuring that the perpetrators would be brought to justice.



He further stated that the Command would deploy all necessary operational and intelligence resources to identify, track and arrest the suspects.



The Police Command expressed its condolences to the families of the deceased officers and the entire police community over the loss, describing the incident as a painful tragedy.



Meanwhile, residents of Abia State have been urged to remain calm and vigilant and to cooperate with security agencies by providing credible information that could assist the ongoing investigation.



The Command advised members of the public to report useful information at the nearest police station, through the suggestion boxes and contact details of Divisional Police Officers provided at police divisions, or through its emergency hotlines: 08035415405, 08079210004, 08079210005 and 08079210006.



Reaffirming its commitment to public safety, the Abia State Police Command urged residents to remain security-conscious, stressing its message: “See Something: Say Something. Together We Keep Abia Safe.”
